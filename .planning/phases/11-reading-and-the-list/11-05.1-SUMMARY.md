# Phase 11 Plan 05.1: The first Space starts no clock; the whole reading and opening do Summary

**The first Space on a message in the list, which reads the subject, the sender and the
snippet, no longer starts the clock towards marking it read. From the build of 2026-09-18
until this branch it did, because 11-05 wrote the moment reading began inside the closure
that composes both forms of the row's text, which runs before `SpaceCycle` has decided which
form this press reads; #25 was reopened the same evening on the tester's answer to the
question 11-05's close comment asked him: reading the snippet is not reading. Now which press
counts is one function, `read_aloud::what_a_press_starts`, a match on the depth the cycle
chose, and `wire_read_aloud` hands the whole reading on to a second closure once the depth is
known; the mail wiring writes the moment there and nowhere else on the Space path, and
opening with Enter writes it as before. A case over the real cycle and the real decision
holds the shape: a first Space marks nothing, a second does, Shift+Space does, a first Space
on another row marks nothing again. The sentence under Mark as read after, the guide and the
changelog say reading the whole message or opening it. Whether the count stays put on the
first Space and moves on the second is the tester's ear; nobody has heard it.**
