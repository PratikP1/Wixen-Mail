<objective>
Put the cursor on the next message after a delete, or on the previous one
when the last was deleted, on the control and not only in the state, and
keep it on the same message when the folder is re-read.

Purpose: #76, the tester on 2026-09-18 under NVDA on `744d05ef`: deleting
a message puts the cursor at the top of the list. The rule he asks for is
already written in `take_row_out_of_the_list`, which sets
`selected_message_index` to the next row or the previous; nothing sets the
control's own focused and selected item to it after the count changes, so
the control reads as the top. And since 10-06 our own expunge wakes the
watch, whose re-read replaces the rows without re-selecting by design, so
the surviving index may sit on a different message.

And #83, added 2026-09-18 as task 3 because it is the same arm: deleting a
message says "Deleting <subject>..." on the key and then, after the
server's round trip, "Deleted: <subject>" or "Moved to Trash: <subject>",
two spoken sentences with the subject in each, and the wait for the second
is a delay in the hand's rhythm. Pratik's decision: say "Delete" and
nothing more; say something only when the delete did not go through.

Output: the rule as a module, the two arms, the reading over a built list,
the delete's sentences to the shape, the changelog entry. Closes #76 and
#83 on the tree side; that NVDA reads the landed row once, and that the
row's reading is enough confirmation by ear, are his ear's. In front of
11-07 because that plan reworks the same function for a set and lands the
cursor after the set through
this rule.
