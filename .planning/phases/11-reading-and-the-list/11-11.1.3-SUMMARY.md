# Phase 11 Plan 11.1.3: All Inboxes keeps a view of its own, and shows every inbox's conversations Summary

**All Inboxes comes up in a view of its own, kept under its own row identity, switched with
Ctrl+T like a folder's and read when it is landed on through Show conversations by default,
rather than in whatever view the last folder left with that folder's conversation rows drawn
under its title: #92's second half, Pratik's decision of 2026-09-20, and the issue is closed.
Showing conversations there lists every inbox's, one row per account and conversation, since
a conversation is an account's; each row carries where it was read and every act on it, the
set a command acts on, the fetch of its text, the tree Enter opens, the count in the tree's
title and the selection held across a switch, reads that account and never the one that
happens to be open. The Thread View check mark says the view of what is on screen everywhere,
and a label and a saved search stay one row per message with Ctrl+T saying so. Nobody has
heard any of it. The every-inbox query costs 107 ms where the message listing costs 18 ms on
16,000 rows over two accounts, after a first draft that cost 198 s.**
