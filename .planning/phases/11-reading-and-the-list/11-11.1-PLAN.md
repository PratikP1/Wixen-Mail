
A `click` event fires on an anchor for a mouse click, for Enter on a
focused link, and for the accessible default action NVDA's Enter in
browse mode performs, since UIA's Invoke on an anchor is a synthesised
click in Chromium. A listener on `document` that calls `preventDefault`
on an anchor click and posts the href therefore catches every way a link
is activated, before any navigation begins, and does not depend on
`WEBVIEW_NAVIGATING` firing or its veto taking effect. That is the route
this plan wires; the veto stays as the second line. Whether the tester's
report is a veto that fires and is ignored, or one that never fires, is
what the probe in task 1 records, and the design above holds under either
answer.
