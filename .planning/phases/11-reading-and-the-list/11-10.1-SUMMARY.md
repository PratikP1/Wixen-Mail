**Task 1.** `application::links_in_text`, `pub mod` in `mod.rs`. `Piece::Text(&str)` and
`Piece::Address { shown, href }`. `addresses_in` walks `split_whitespace` with each word's

word, and keeps every character in exactly one piece. `the_address_in` peels the brackets and
quotes that open the word, takes off what closes it through `without_what_closes_it`, which

its href as a double-quoted attribute; `spoken` says "link to" `the_host_of` the href, the
authority up to a `/`, `?` or `#` with the port and a `www.` off, and a mail address up to its
