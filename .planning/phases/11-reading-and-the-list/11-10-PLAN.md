<objective>
Let a rule change how a row is announced and not only how it looks: a
phrase said first, an optional sound once per check, and the labels heard
on the row as a column.

Purpose: #62, from the Outlook gap report of 2026-08-27 and the audit of
2026-09-15: conditional formatting in the sense that matters here is
absent. `FilterAction` has nothing that changes a row's speech, the row
takes no per-message override, and a label a rule puts on a message is not
heard on the row because there is no Labels column.

the editor, the readings, the pages. Closes #62 on the tree side; what a
phrase sounds like at the start of a row, and the sound, are the tester's.

columns, the sound scheme, the editor and the window. It stays one plan

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| a rule's phrase to speech | a phrase spoken at the start of every matching row is a phrase spoken hundreds of times in a folder of matches |
| a rule's sound to the check | a sound per match is a flood; one per check is the bound |
| the phrase on the message to the listing | a stored phrase is text a person typed, shown in a cell and never interpreted |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-11-36 | Denial of service | a sound per matched message | high | mitigate | one signal per check from the `WhatArrived` arm with the count; the reading holds it out of the folder loop; a record measures the break |
| T-11-37 | Denial of service | a long phrase said before every row | medium | mitigate | the phrase bounded to 40 characters at the reader; the column can be switched off, the prefix cannot, which the guide says |
| T-11-38 | Tampering | a phrase read as markup or a command | low | mitigate | plain text in a cell through `cell_text`; nothing parses it |
| T-11-39 | Information disclosure | a phrase logged | low | mitigate | the check logs counts; 11-04's guard reads it |
| T-11-SC | Tampering | npm, pip or cargo installs | high | mitigate | no crate is added to `Cargo.toml`; the summary says so |
</threat_model>
