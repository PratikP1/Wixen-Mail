# Phase 11 Plan 09.2: A snippet is the message's first relevant words Summary

`application::snippet`, Pratik's decision of 2026-09-18 on #82, and not cut from the text at
200 characters: the signature after its delimiter is left out through `sign_off::split`,
quoted lines are left out, addresses are dropped altogether with the brackets round one going

#82 is closed from the merge with the ear list.**
