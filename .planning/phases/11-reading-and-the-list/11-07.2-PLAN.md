<objective>
A move or a copy to a folder on another account completes here first,
the way a move within one account does after 11-07.1.

Purpose: #86 and Pratik's comment of 2026-09-19 overruling 11-07.1's
decision 29: a move across two accounts completes here first as well,
the message's bytes held here on the outbox's shape so the crossing is
replayed from the row after a restart, the fetch at one server and the
append at the other done in the background or at the next check, the
row moved at once, a refusal at either server undoing it here and said.
Most of the shape is already in the tree: `moves_in_flight` holds a
message's bytes while it moves, `move_it_across` appends before it
removes and asks the destination whether the identifier landed when the
append's answer never came, and `finish_the_move` resumes from held
bytes. What is not there is the row leaving before the servers answer,
the crossing running in the background and at a check rather than in
front of the person, and a restart replaying without a question.

Output: the queue's kind for a crossing, the crossing split into steps
a held row can resume from, the arms completing here first, the replay
at a check of either account, the ceiling path with its sentence, the
readings, the privacy page, the guide and the changelog. Closes the
second half of #86's move; #63's copy and move across accounts are
re-taken after it, and ledger 187's three questions stay the tester's.
</objective>
