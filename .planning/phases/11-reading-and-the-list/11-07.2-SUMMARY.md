# Phase 11 Plan 07.2: A move or a copy to another account completes here first as well Summary

**A move or a copy of a message to a folder on another account now happens on this computer
first, on Pratik's decision of 2026-09-19 overruling 11-07.1's decision 29. The row leaves the
list the moment the folder is chosen, "Moved to Work in Home: subject" is shown and spoken by
nothing, the message sits in the other account's folder here at once, and the crossing is a row
in `moves_waiting` with the other account named, its bytes in the store phase 4.1 wrote for a
crossing interrupted by a restart. Behind it the crossing runs in the three steps
`mail_across_accounts` is now cut into, fetch and keep, append and ask, remove at the source, in
that order, on both accounts' sessions in the background and again at the next check of either
account before that account's first listing; a restart resumes from the held bytes by asking the
destination first, without the question phase 4.1 used to put. A refusal at either server comes
back as `MovePutBack`, whose arm puts the row back where the server holds it, lets the bytes go
and speaks the reason at High; an append nobody could settle is not reached and never a refusal;
a message the source would not let go once the destination had it is said to be in both places.
A message over 25 MB, or of a size nobody knows, cannot be held here and goes the old way,
server first through the worker, with "larger than 25 MB, so it goes now and the row leaves when
Home has taken it" spoken. A copy across accounts is a marked copy in the other account's folder
at once, as a copy within the account is. Nobody has heard a row leave for another account, and
no real server has met this.**
