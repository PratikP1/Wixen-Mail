<objective>
Give the folder tree its shape: one branch per account, folders nested under their
parents, local folders under a group of their own, and a memory of what was
collapsed that survives a rename.

The tree is one flat level today, rebuilt whole on every sync. `UIUpdate::FoldersLoaded`
already carries the convention this plan follows twice over — a named branch,
filled, expanded, and omitted entirely when empty rather than left as an empty
node to arrow into — and it already carries the written statement of the defect
D-25 fixes: a saved search keeps its row through a rebuild even when its name has
just changed, because what is open is held as the row's path while
`land_the_cursor` matches on the row's words. Every new row type this phase adds
needs the identity fix, not the label match.

Purpose: roadmap criteria 2 and 3, and the surface every later plan in this phase
hangs a row on.

Output: a pure `folder_tree` module that decides the rows, an identity carried
beside every row, a `tree_state` table remembering what was collapsed, and a
rebuilt `FoldersLoaded`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries
