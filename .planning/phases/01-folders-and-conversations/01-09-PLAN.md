<objective>
Empty a folder, and mark one read, with the two settings that decide how far each
reaches.

D-33 is the load-bearing decision: emptying deletes every message through the same
decision one deletion uses, `local_folders::deleting(from, protocol, asked, allowed)`,
rather than writing a second answer to what deleting means. So emptying Trash
removes, emptying Inbox moves to Trash, and the per-account "Let me delete mail on
this computer" setting gates it without a second gate being written. The
confirmation can say which of the two will happen, because it knows.

D-34 and D-35 are kept as two settings deliberately: one destroys mail, the other
loses your place, and neither has an undo.

Purpose: the last of roadmap criterion 1, and two of the five settings in
criterion 8.

Output: an `emptying` module, two settings offered and read, two commands with
confirmations that carry the whole cost.
</objective>
