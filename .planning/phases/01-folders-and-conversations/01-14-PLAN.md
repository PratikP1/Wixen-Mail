<objective>
Close roadmap success criterion 3, the one gap the phase verification found.

`folder_tree::rows` is multi-account throughout and 01-05 proved D-13 as a
property of it. Its single caller, at `wx_app.rs:9467`, hands it a slice
containing exactly one `AccountInTheTree` built from one `account_id`, and
`UIUpdate::FoldersLoaded` wipes the tree with `delete_all_items` before every
rebuild. So the tree never holds two accounts at once and the criterion's own
test, two POP accounts showing distinguishable Inboxes, cannot happen.

This is the last thing between phase 1 and its eight criteria.
</objective>

<why_now_rather_than_a_later_phase>
Pratik chose this over rewriting the criterion, on performance grounds, and the
reasoning is worth carrying because it decides what "done" looks like.

Today, moving between accounts tears the whole tree down and rebuilds it. That
rebuild runs at least five cache reads: `get_tags_for_account`,
`folders_in_the_tree`, `folder_parents`, `what_the_server_said`, `pinned_rows`,
and the collapsed state below them. `MessageCache` wraps a non-`Sync` connection
and every local call runs on the interface thread, so each rebuild is work on
the thread that draws. Twelve call sites trigger it.

Held multi-account, that cost moves from once-per-switch to once-at-load, and
traversing accounts becomes arrow keys inside a tree that already exists. The
trade is an initial build larger by the other accounts' folders, paid once, with
branches starting collapsed.

**So a solution that rebuilds the whole tree on every account switch anyway has
not done the job**, even if it renders every account. The point is that the
switch stops being a rebuild.
</why_now_rather_than_a_later_phase>

<what_earlier_plans_already_settled>
Do not redo any of this; check it holds.

- `folder_tree::rows(accounts, folders, parents, pins, ...)` takes a slice of
  accounts and is tested multi-account (01-05).
- Each account's local Sent, Outbox, Drafts, Junk and Trash are now shared under
  a reserved owner (01-07), so showing every account at once no longer lists the
  same Drafts once per account. That was the blocker, and it shipped.
- Collapsed state, pins and gone-marks are all keyed by identity rather than by
  label (01-05, 01-08, 01-10), so they survive a tree that holds more rows.
- `land_the_cursor` puts the cursor back after a rebuild and exists because a
  sync on a timer used to move it mid-list.
</what_earlier_plans_already_settled>

<action>
Make `folder_tree_updates` gather every account rather than one. For each
account, read its folders, parents, tags and server-said state as it does now,
then call `folder_tree::rows` once with every `AccountInTheTree` and the union
of the per-account data. `pinned_rows` and the collapsed state are already read
once for the whole tree; keep them that way.

Keep the per-account reads per account. Do not invent a combined query: five
reads times N accounts is the honest cost, it happens once at load rather than
per switch, and a new all-accounts query would be a second answer to a question
`folders_in_the_tree` already owns.

Preserve `land_the_cursor`. A tree holding more rows makes losing the cursor
worse, not better.
</action>
