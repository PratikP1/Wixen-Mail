<objective>
Keep a browser sign-in's token away from any server that is not the
provider's, and keep it only when the person who consented is the
account it was asked for.

Purpose: Pratik's request of 2026-10-14 carries R18 of the audit,
finding #18, and the unreported #23. A Gmail token carries full read,
delete and send access, and today it is handed to whatever IMAP or SMTP
host the account names: a typo of Gmail's host, or a non-Google relay
set as the outgoing server, receives it if it asks. And no sign-in
tells Google or Microsoft which address to sign in as or checks who
consented, so one wrong pick in the browser's account chooser files
another person's token under this account, and the calendar, contacts
and tasks syncs then read and write that person's data.

Requirements: GAP-06, under which 13-44.5 built `WhoRunsTheMail` and
both halves of the browser sign-in ask it, and REAL-01, whose 14-03
line built the separate Google sign-in for calendars, contacts and
tasks. A dated line under each, adding no `[D]` clause and touching no
box; REAL-01 stays phase 14's to tick.

Output: the servers' check, the login hint, the Google identity gate,
the sentences, the privacy page's line, T-13-PK-02 moved to mitigate by
dating, the ledger entries, the pull request.
</objective>
