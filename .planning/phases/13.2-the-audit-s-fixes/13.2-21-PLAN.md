<objective>
Make a full read of an address book or a Google calendar take off what the
provider deleted while the stored marker was stale, make the Google calendar
read in full only when Google says the marker expired, and make the contact
syncs read the address book once per sync instead of once per person.

Purpose: audit findings #29 and #30, both unreported to Pratik and graded
"should" by their verdicts; his request of 2026-10-14 is every item that
needs fixing. A laptop shut for two weeks comes back to cancelled meetings
that still alert and deleted contacts that never leave, and a first sync of
3,000 contacts decodes about 4.5 million rows. The CardDAV sync never takes
off a card its server dropped, on any read, which the audit did not name and
which this plan fixes beside the other three for the same reason.

Output: the 410-only fallback, the passes after a full answer for the Google
calendar and the three contact syncs, the snapshot, the cases, the guard
records, the changelog, the ledger, REAL-01's dated line and the four marks.
</objective>
