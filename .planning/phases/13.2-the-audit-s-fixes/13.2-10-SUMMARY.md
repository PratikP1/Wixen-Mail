# Phase 13.2 Plan 10: One check of an account at a time, and a lighter flag pass Summary

A second check of one account now waits for the first and then finds nothing new and says nothing, so new mail is announced once with one sound and the rules run on it once; different accounts are still checked side by side, and a check's flag pass reads the account and its keyword labels once rather than once a message.

## What changed

**Finding #15.** `mail_session::one_check_at_a_time(account_id)` finds the account's lock in `the_turns_being_held`, a `OnceLock` map of `Arc<tokio::sync::Mutex<()>>` beside `the_sessions_being_held`, lets the map's lock go, and waits for `lock_owned()`. `spawn_mail_sync`'s per-account loop takes it as its first statement, `let _this_accounts_turn = handle.block_on(...)`, before the POP branch and `the_session_at(`, and holds it to the end of the pass. `git grep -n "one_check_at_a_time" -- src/presentation/wx_app.rs` names one line, `:31445`, inside `spawn_mail_sync`, above its `the_session_at(` at `:31491`. A second check then reads `stored_uids` after the first has written, fetches nothing, and `what_arrived` gives `None`, so no `WhatArrived` is sent (premise 2, read and not changed).

**Finding #14.** In `sync_folder`'s flag pass, before the loop: the account through `account_of_folder` once (a failure logged, and the pass writes the server's flags as they came, as before), `the_changes_waiting_by_row(cache, account)` taking the account rather than looking it up, and `labels_with_a_keyword` once. In the loop, the row looked up once (`mail_sync.rs:2034`, the only `message_row_for_uid(` between the loop's head at `:2033` and its end) and used for the waiting change and the labels; label matching through `match_these_labels_to_keywords` only when a label carries a keyword. `message_row_for_uid` and `set_message_flags` run on `prepare_cached`. No transaction around the loop (premise 3). `match_labels_to_keywords` keeps its signature and calls the two new functions.

**`folder_account`** is deleted in its own refactor commit. `git grep -n "folder_account(" -- src` named six lines before the green (two calls in `mail_sync.rs`, the definition, three in the test) and none after the refactor. Its doc comment had been fused with `folder_kind`'s and a stray line of `get_folder`'s sat on `account_of_folder`; each now has its own.
