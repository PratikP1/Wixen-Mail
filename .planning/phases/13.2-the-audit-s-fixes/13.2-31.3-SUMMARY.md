# Phase 13.2 Plan 31.3: The Outbox's Delete keeps a message being sent Summary

Delete in the Outbox no longer takes out a message a pass of the send loop has claimed and says
"It will not be sent." while it goes: the store refuses a claimed row in its one delete, and the
window says once, after its loop, "One message is being sent now, so it stays in the Outbox." or
the same with their number. Ledger 843, on Pratik's yes of 2026-10-15.

## What works now

- `cancel_queued` runs `DELETE ... WHERE rowid = ?1 AND sending_since IS NULL`; when nothing came
  out, one `SELECT EXISTS` on the rowid answers `BeingSent` or `NotThere`. A failed send lets go
  of its claim (13.2-04), so the next Delete takes that message out.
- `cancel_if_queued` hands back `InTheOutbox`: not the Outbox, answered, or being sent. It says
  nothing for one being sent; `delete_these` counts those, leaves them out of `reached`, and
  says the words once through `send_refusal` after the loop.
- Undo Send's too-late answer: "Too late to take that back. Look in the Outbox: if the message is
  still there and is not being sent, Delete takes it out of the queue."
- CI, NVDA and Accessibility on pull request 192: see the merge commit's message.
