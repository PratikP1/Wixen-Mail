<objective>
Stop the download of everything asking forever for text that always fails,
and stop it re-reading the whole open folder and speaking its count after
every chunk.

Purpose: finding #11 (reported as R9) and finding #36 (not reported).
Nothing records a message whose text failed, and a chunk ends early only
after three failures in a row, so one or two messages that always fail are
asked for again with no wait, two round trips and two queries over the
whole account each turn, a progress line each turn, until a check forgets
them, which can be six hours or never. Three such messages together end the
run for every account after this one. And each chunk of 500 headers makes
the interface thread re-read the whole open folder, which grows with the
square of the folder, and in flat view speaks "N messages, M unread" each
time, which guardrail 5 forbids.

Output: the run's memory of what it asked, the per-account end, the
throttled re-read, the reason on MessagesLoaded, the readings, the
changelog, the ledger, the four marks, and the pull request.
</objective>
