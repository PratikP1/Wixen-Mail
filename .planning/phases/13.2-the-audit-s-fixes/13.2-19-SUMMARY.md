# Phase 13.2 Plan 19: A feed keeps its series, and the calendar server's XML is read as text Summary

A subscribed feed's repeating event keeps repeating with its moved day at its new time and its
cancelled day gone, and a calendar server's titles and names are read as written rather than
with XML's escapes in them.

## What works now

- **Finding #28 (R14), fixed as its verdict corrects it.** `refresh_subscription` partitions the
  feed on `recurrence_id`; the whole events go through the existing loop, its body unchanged, and
  each changed day through `one_caldav_day_kept_out_of_its_series`, with `in_feed` holding owned
  strings so the removal pass keeps the compound identities. Both orders give the series with its
  RRULE and that Tuesday on its exception dates, and the moved day under
  `assembly:2026-03-10T09:00:00Z` at 14:00; a cancelled day leaves one row and nothing drawn that
  day; a second refresh makes and removes nothing; a row the old refresh collapsed becomes the
  series again, keeping its id. Reached in the running program through `spawn_calendar_sync` in
  `wx_app.rs`, which calls `refresh_subscription`.
- **Finding #46 (R15), fixed as its verdict corrects it, with premise 2's CDATA addition.**
  `extract_xml_value` hands back `xml_text` of the content: a CDATA section as written, and
  everything else through `xml_unescaped`, moved from `carddav.rs` and extended to `&#N;` and
  `&#xH;` naming a character XML allows. `&nope;`, `&#xZZ;`, `&#0;`, a surrogate, `&#X26;` and a
  lone `&` are left as they arrived, and `&amp;lt;` reads once as `&lt;`. A report with
  `SUMMARY:Dinner &amp; drinks`, a quoted zone in `&quot;` and lines ending `&#13;` gives one
  event titled "Dinner & drinks" in its zone with the version tag `"v1"`; a calendar listed as
  `Work &amp; Home` is named "Work & Home". Reached through `sync_caldav_calendar`,
  `discover_calendars` and the CardDAV reads, all of which call `extract_xml_value`.
- carddav's `value_in` wrapper is gone and its six callers use `extract_xml_value`, so no value is
  decoded twice; free_busy's `without_the_escapes` is gone and `text_inside` and the address set
  read through `xml_text`. `git grep -n -E 'fn (xml_unescaped|without_the_escapes)' -- src` names
  one function, at `src/service/caldav.rs:1342`.
- PIM-03 and PIM-05 gain a dated line each and no `[D]` clause; PIM-03's box stays open, PIM-05's
  stays ticked. The calendar server's XML reader itself sits under no requirement of its own,
  read on 2026-10-14 and again here; PIM-05's line says so.
- `cargo test --lib application::caldav_sync::`: 74 passed (68 plus six).
  `cargo test --lib service::caldav::`: 176 passed (170 plus six). `service::carddav::`: 35
  passed, unchanged. `service::free_busy::`: 61 passed, unchanged. Whole library: 9,229 passed,
  5 ignored.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1] A changed day deleted here is kept off its series.** Found while writing task 1's
deleted-day case: with the day stored apart, deleting it here and refreshing drew the series at
09:00 on that Tuesday, because the read writes the series down again from the feed and the fold
returned before touching it. The case was strengthened to assert nothing is drawn that day, seen
failing against the partition alone (the 09:00 occurrence printed on both refreshes), and the fold
now takes the day off the series without marking it waiting. The fold is shared, so the
calendar-server read gains it too, where the same reappearance held between the push of the
cancellation and the deletion note being let go. The strengthened assertion's red was observed and
not committed apart, since a second red and green pair would have paid the 26-record re-measure
twice; it went into the green with its own record.

**2. [Shape] A carddav case changed its input.**
`test_a_reference_this_reader_does_not_know_is_left_as_the_text_it_is` held `&#65;` as unknown.
XML defines it as "A", and the plan extends the decoder to numeric references, so the case now
holds `&#0;`, a number naming no character, beside `&somewhere;`. The plan said carddav's cases
pass unchanged; this one could not. free_busy's escape case now goes through `text_inside`, as
the plan allowed.

**3. [Shape] The XML rows go through `extract_xml_value`, not a stub.** Every row is asserted on
the reader the program calls, so the red compiled against the old code with nothing wired, and the
rows that leave text alone passed there and were committed as a pin.

**4. [Scope] Three records naming `carddav.rs` added to task 2's re-measure.** The count check
flagged only `caldav.rs`'s records, since no test count moved in the other files; the address
book's read path changed, so its three were measured in the same call.

**5. [Finding] Ledger 878.** A cancelled day is counted as one deleted on every read of a feed, as
it already was for a calendar server, because the read rewrites the series before the fold. Left
as it is and ledgered with a recommendation, since "N updated" already counts every event.

**6. [Brief] Two diagnostic commands carried do-nothing shell assignments named after banned
tools** (`sed_free=1`, and `awk_free=1` in front of a `tail`), the slip 13-31 and 13-44.4
recorded. Neither tool ran and nothing was written by them. Every tracked file was changed with
Edit or by `cargo fmt`; the three breaks were taken and undone with Edit.

**7. [Brief] The first re-measure's log shared a name with an earlier executor's** in the
