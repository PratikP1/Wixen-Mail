<objective>
Keep a subscribed feed's repeating events and their changed days apart,
and read a calendar server's XML as the text it carries.

Purpose: Pratik's request of 2026-10-14 carries R14 and R15 of the
audit. #28: a feed carries a series and its moved or cancelled days as
separate VEVENTs sharing one UID, and the refresh saves each under that
UID, so the table's unique key collapses them onto one row and whichever
comes last wins: either a whole weekly series becomes one event, or a
moved meeting shows at its old time and a cancelled one still shows. #46:
the calendar's XML reader never takes off XML's escaping, so "Dinner &
drinks" is stored and spoken as "Dinner &amp; drinks", and editing it
writes the escape back to the server for every other client to show.

Requirements: PIM-03 (an exception to a series shows on the date it
really is, open), which #28 makes true for subscribed feeds, and PIM-05
(CardDAV for contacts), whose decoder becomes the shared one. A dated
line under each, adding no `[D]` clause and touching no box. The
calendar server's reader itself sits under no requirement of its own
(read on 2026-10-14), and the summary says so.

Output: the split refresh and its cases, the one decoder and its rows,
the two decoders gone, the changelog, the ledger entries, the pull
request.
</objective>

**Where the audit was wrong.** It said two rows are stored under the
same id on the first refresh. The unique key makes it one row from the
start, written over by each VEVENT in turn: series first and the row
becomes the one day, losing the repeat; changed day first and the move
or cancellation is lost. The verdict's fix is kept: partition, save the
whole events in the existing loop, fold each changed day with the CalDAV
read's function, with `in_feed` holding owned strings so the compound
ids keep the removal pass from deleting the changed days. Because the
fold is called with the provider itself as who took the day out, the
series is not marked waiting, and because `carry_over_local_only` does
not carry `provider_recurrence_id`, a row the old refresh collapsed is
put right by the series VEVENT at the next refresh. No subscription test
uses RECURRENCE-ID today.

The verdict's fix is kept: one decoder in caldav.rs as `pub(crate)`,

back exactly as written, with the wrapper taken off and nothing inside
it decoded; today no CDATA is handled anywhere, so a server that wraps
calendar-data in one is read wrongly now and would be read wrongly in a
new way once decoding is on.
