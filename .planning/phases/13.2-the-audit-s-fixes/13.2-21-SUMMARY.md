# Phase 13.2 Plan 21: A full read says what has gone Summary

A whole read of a Google calendar, of Google or Outlook contacts, and every CardDAV read whose
cards could all be read now takes off what the provider deleted; the Google calendar reads
whole only when Google answers 410; and the three contact syncs read the address book once per
sync instead of once per person.

## What works now

- **Finding #29, the Google calendar.** `read_one_google_calendar` works out one window from
  `the_stretch_a_read_asks_about`, six months back and a year ahead from the calendar server's
  two named distances, and that one window is what a whole read asks for and what the pass
  after it covers. After a whole read, `take_off_what_the_provider_no_longer_holds` drops the
  rows of that calendar inside the window that the answer did not name, by
  `WhatTheAnswerCovers::OnlyBetween`, `would_have_named` and `may_be_taken_off_this_computer`.
  Kept: a change waiting, a row deleted here, a row outside the window, a series starting
  before it, a day of a series the answer named, a row of another calendar, and what this
  sync's push just sent. A read from a marker takes nothing off.
- **Finding #29 (a).** The fallback calls `contacts_sync::is_expired_sync_token`, now
  `pub(crate)`, so only a 410 reads the calendar whole; any other failure is returned, the main
  calendar's failing the sync and a listed calendar's said in the summary, with the marker kept.
  Google's guide "Synchronize resources efficiently", last updated 2026-09-11 and read
  2026-10-17: "the server will respond to an incremental request with a response code 410.
  This should trigger a full wipe of the client's store and a new full sync."
- **Finding #29, the contacts, and the CardDAV gap.** After a whole Google read (no marker, or
  after an expired one), a whole Outlook read (no link, or after an expired one), and every
  CardDAV read with `could_not_be_read` at 0, `take_off_who_a_whole_answer_left_out` reads each
  person this address book knows again by id and, if the answer left her out, carries it out
  through `one_address_book_deleted_them`: dropped if nobody else holds her, kept without this
  address book if another does. A person with a change waiting or held for a choice stays and is
  counted as nothing.
- **Finding #30.** `TheContactsHere` reads the account once per sync and is kept in step: a
  merge and a create write their result into it, and a deletion writes what
  `one_address_book_deleted_them` now answers it left. The matched contact is read again by id
  through the new `MessageCache::get_contact` just before her fate is decided. The read count
  for two people and for two hundred is the same in each sync (it was 4 and 202). The
  remaining whole-account reads outside the tests, by `git grep -n get_contacts_for_account`
  on the branch: `contacts_sync.rs:1009` (`TheContactsHere::read`, once per sync in each of
  the three), `contacts_sync.rs:2065` (`changes_waiting_for`, once per push) and

  `contacts_sync.rs` :1862, :2404, :2416, :2495, :2623, :2635, :2708 and `carddav_sync.rs`
  :290, :384 on `main`.

## Deviations from Plan

whose records take about three hours to re-measure; split, they would have been re-measured
twice. 13.2-17's decision for the same file.

**2. [Rule 2] What the Google push just sent counts as named.** `push_to_google` now hands back
Google's identities for what it took, as `push_to_microsoft` does since 13.2-18, so a meeting
made in this sync is not taken off by a whole read that does not carry it yet. Held by
`test_a_meeting_this_sync_sent_to_google_is_not_taken_off_by_its_read` and its record.

**3. [Shape] The Outlook pass is shared, not copied.** `take_off_what_outlook_no_longer_holds`
became `take_off_what_the_provider_no_longer_holds`, and `the_stretch_a_round_reads` became
`the_stretch_a_read_asks_about`, used for the Google window; the named distances are read
through it rather than directly.

**4. [Scope] `src/common/answering.rs`, a file the plan did not list,** gains
`answering_each_with_its_own_status`, a stand-in giving each reply its own status, with its
own case; `answering_as_asked` now calls it.

**5. [Shape] The re-read is held at the snapshot's level.** The loops make no network call
between reading the contacts and deciding, so no stand-in can make an edit land inside one.

it read, replaced in the green; the two cases ask its `the_one_this_is` directly.

**6. [Shape] The snapshot is read after the answer arrives, and the create passes read it as

unchanged.
