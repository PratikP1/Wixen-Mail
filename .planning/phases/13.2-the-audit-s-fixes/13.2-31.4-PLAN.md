<objective>
When Send cannot take a sent draft's copy out of the server's Drafts folder, remember the
removal and try it again at that account's next check for mail, silent unless it fails
again.

Purpose: 13.2-05 made Send take a sent draft's filed copy out of Drafts. When the server
cannot be reached or refuses, it says so once and never tries again, so a message sent
while offline leaves its copy in Drafts on every device, where somebody can send it a
second time. That is ledger 845, and Pratik answered yes to its recommendation on
2026-10-15.

Output: the kept removals and their table, the second try in the check, the sentences,
the cases and readings, the changelog, ledger 845 closed and a row for the ear, the four
marks, and the pull request.
</objective>

`spawn_draft_removal` (`wx_app.rs:20234-20266`) opens a session with `a_session_at`, and a
session it cannot open is mapped to `TakenAway::ACopyMayStillBeThere(reason)`, the same
answer as a server's refusal; it says `what_happened()` on the status line when
`needs_saying()` and keeps nothing. 13.2-05's summary, known limits: "A removal that fails
is not retried (ledger 845)." Ledger 845 (`.planning/WINDOWS.md:862`) recommends keeping
the Message-ID and trying again "on the next check for mail of that account, saying
nothing unless it fails again".

**2. Where the second try belongs in a check.** `spawn_mail_sync`
(`wx_app.rs:31464-31919`) opens the account's session at `:31576`, replays the moves that
were waiting at `:31593`, then `send_the_flag_changes_that_were_waiting` at `:31610`, "the

conversation ids at `:31618-31637`, then lists the folders at `:31638`. The second try goes
right after the flag changes and before the folders are listed, in the same shape: on
this check's session, so the Drafts folder this check reads no longer holds the copy. A
check that cannot open the session ends before it (`:31579`), so a server still out
of reach costs a kept removal nothing and says nothing more than the check already says.
POP accounts take `check_pop_mail` at `:31536` and never reach it; their Drafts folder is
on this computer, where 13.2-05's removal does not fail for want of a server. The check's
session is an `Arc<MailController>` (`mail_session.rs:127`), and `DraftAtTheServer` wraps
a `&MailController` (`draft_copy.rs:249-251`).

**3. What the kept removal must hold, and the guard it needs.** The second try needs the
account, the Drafts folder's path, the draft's Message-ID (`draft_message::message_id_for`
of the draft's id, `wx_app.rs:20057`), the draft's id and its subject: the id to check that
the draft is not a draft here again before anything is removed (`load_draft`, as the Send
arm reads it at `:19797`), and the subject because a sentence said at a later check has
no context and has to name the message. Undo Send files a taken-back message as a new
draft with a new id (`wx_app.rs:25050`, `save_draft_row(app, cache, &taken_back, None)`),
so the guard is a safety net rather than the common case.

**4. Kept before the spawn, forgotten by the worker.** A removal written down only after
it failed would be lost if the program closed mid-removal, which is the offline case at
its likeliest. So it is kept on the interface thread before `spawn_draft_removal` starts,
and the worker forgets it when the answer is `Gone` or `NothingWasFiled`, through a cache
of its own, the way the Empty Folder worker opens one (`wx_app.rs:10477-10481`); the
window's cache cannot cross threads (`delete_if_local`'s doc, `:27873-27879`). A removal
kept while a check is also trying it is harmless: both take `one_filing_at_a_time`, and
the second finds nothing and forgets.

copy went or none was there; when it failed again, one sentence for the check, naming the
subject for one message and the number for several, then the removal is forgotten, so a
server that refuses for good is said once and not at every check (guardrail 5). A log
line names the account and the folder and never the subject (CLAUDE.md, never a subject in
