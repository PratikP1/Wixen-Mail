So narrowing alone (A, B) is not enough: it works only when the store has
statistics, which premise 4 cannot promise. C and D both hold. D is taken:
SQLite's page on `INDEXED BY` says it "is not intended for use in tuning the
performance of a query" and that "careful placement of unary + operators is
a better method for controlling which indices are used by a query"
(sqlite.org/lang_indexedby.html, read 2026-10-15); its section 8.1 of
optoverview.html calls the plus a work-around, which is why the comment
beside it says what was measured and why the planner could not be trusted to
choose here. `INDEXED BY` would also break the listing guard: its stripped
database (`messages.rs:8663`) copies tables and no indexes, and the index list
cannot simply be copied with them, since `idx_messages_inline_body` names a
column the guard drops. The plus drops type affinity (section 8.1); both sides
of each term are integer folder ids, so nothing compares differently. No new
index: a composite on the conversation and the folder would add a write to
every insert (ledger 838) for nothing these figures ask for. The whole
listing keeps its text and its folder index, which is the right plan for
reading a whole folder.
