# Phase 13.2 Plan 31.4: A sent draft's copy left at the server goes at the next check Summary

When Send cannot take a sent draft's copy out of the server's Drafts folder, the removal is
now kept and tried again at that account's next check for mail, silent when that works and
said once, naming the message, when it fails again. On the same branch, ledger 854: the
removal takes only the messages whose Message-ID is the draft's exactly.

## What works now

- `take_the_filed_copy_away` keeps a `DraftLeftAtTheServer` (account, draft id, Message-ID,
  folder, subject, time) before `spawn_draft_removal` starts. The worker forgets it through a
  cache of its own when the answer is `Gone` or `NothingWasFiled`, and keeps it otherwise.
- Every IMAP check calls `take_away_the_drafts_left_at_the_server` right after
  `send_the_flag_changes_that_were_waiting` and before `fetch_folders`, on its own session.
  For each kept removal: a draft that `load_draft` finds is forgotten with nothing removed;
  otherwise it takes `one_filing_at_a_time()`, calls `take_away_the_filed_copy`, and forgets
  the removal whatever the answer. The copies still there are said once after the loop.
- Send's sentence: "The message you sent may still be in Drafts on the server, so your other
  devices could show it as a draft nobody has sent. Wixen Mail tries to take it out again at
  the next check for mail. The server said: ..."
- A second failure: "The message "Lunch on Friday" you sent is still in Drafts on the server,
  ... Taking it out failed again, so delete it from Drafts yourself. The server said: ...", or
  "3 messages you sent are still in Drafts ...". The log names the account id and folder and
  the server's words, never the subject.
- `clear_drafts`, which `delete_account` calls, deletes the account's kept removals.
- Ledger 854: `take_away_the_filed_copy` reads each number the search answers with
  `KeepsTheDraft::identifier_at` (`MailController::message_id_at` underneath) and keeps it
  only when `renumbered::the_number_confirmed` says it is the draft's identifier.
- CI, NVDA and Accessibility on pull request 193: see the merge commit's message.

## Known limits

- If keeping the removal fails (a database write refused), Send still says it will be tried
  again; the failure is logged and the next check has nothing to try.
- A server that refuses for good is said once at the next check and then forgotten; the person
  deletes the copy by hand.
- Saving a newer copy of a draft removes the older copies the search finds without the exact
  reading (ledger 902).
- Nothing has met a real server; nobody has heard the sentences (ledger 901).
