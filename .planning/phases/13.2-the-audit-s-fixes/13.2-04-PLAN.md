<objective>
Make the Outbox claim a message before it sends it, so two passes of the
send loop never send the same message, and Undo Send never says "Taken
back" about a message that is on its way.

Purpose: findings #31 and #6 of the audit of 2026-10-14 are one defect
seen from two sides: reading a queued row does not claim it. With the
default ten-second hold, two messages coming due a few seconds apart start
two passes, and the second sends the first again while the first pass is
still signing in. And Undo Send treats a delete that found the row as proof
it caught the message, when a pass may already hold it in memory. Pratik
asked on 2026-10-14 for every item the audit found to be fixed as a phase.

Output: a claim column and its methods, the send loop and start-up using
them, the corrected comments, the readings, the changelog, the ledger entry
for the ear, the four marks, and the pull request.
</objective>

The doc at `outbox.rs:384-391` and the comment at `wx_app.rs:24317-24322`
say an `Ok(false)` from Undo's delete is how it finds out it lost a race
with the send loop. It cannot be: the loop removes a row only after its
send finished, so while a send is in progress the row is still there and
the delete answers true. Both comments describe a protection that does not
exist, and are corrected here.

**2. The audit was wrong on two counts, and the verdicts corrected it.**

- #31 said each copy gets a fresh Message-ID. It does not:
  `mail_controller.rs:248` builds it with `message_id::derived(&req.queue_id,
  &req.from_address)`, so both copies carry one identifier. SMTP still
  delivers both; Gmail may fold them, Outlook and Thunderbird show two, and
  Sent holds two.
- #6's case of the one-second tick interleaving with Undo cannot happen: the
  tick (`:6518-6536`) and `undo_send` (called at `:6008`) both run on the interface
  thread. The real case is a pass already started by another trigger (the
  six callers of `flush_outbox(app)` at `:6005, :6012, :6535, :16836,
  :19319, :31844`) whose `now`, taken after its own database open at
  `:24554`, lands just past the message's moment while Undo is saving its
  draft.

**3. #31's own fix is not used, and why.** Its `AtomicBool` pair loses a
request between the last check and the clear, stays set after a panic, and
needs clearing on each of the five early returns at `:24552, :24563,
:24575, :24600, :24609`. Its verdict's alternative, a `tokio` mutex around
the whole pass, makes a second message wait for the first pass to finish
and does nothing for Undo Send. #6's claim column closes both, as its
verdict says, and is the one fix taken: the claim is the arbiter between
two passes, between a pass and Undo Send, and between two copies of the
program, which `main.rs:136-142` says share one outbox.
