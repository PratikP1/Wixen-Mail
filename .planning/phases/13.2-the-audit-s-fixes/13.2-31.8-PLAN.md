<objective>
Give the import its own true sentence for a mailbox file inside a zip that holds more than
the archive says it does, the case zip 9.0.4 now catches partway through a file, and keep
bringing in everything honest the archive holds.

Purpose: measured by 13.2-31.7's planner on zip 9.0.4, a zip whose one mailbox file of
394,670 bytes is recorded as 100,000 imports nothing and says "No folders were imported. 1
message in the archive could not be read, because there was nothing in it a mail program
recognises." Recorded as 300,000 beside a second, honest mailbox file, it brings in 878 of the
file's 2,000 messages and all three of the honest file's, and says "1 message in the archive
could not be read", so somebody is told one message failed while 1,122 are missing. Both
sentences are false, and the second hides most of a folder. Pratik answered yes to this plan
on 2026-10-17/18. 13.2-31.7 gave the whole-archive case its sentence; this plan gives the
partway case one in the same words, through the typed reason 13.2-31.7 made.

Output: the reader's typed answer, the import's count and sentence, the cases, the changelog,
a ledger row opened and fixed, a row for what is left, the four marks, and the pull request.
