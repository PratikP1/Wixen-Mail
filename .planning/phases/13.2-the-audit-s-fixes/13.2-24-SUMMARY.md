  reads every `.yml` under `.github/workflows` through `every_workflow_with_its_name` and

  the red it named release.yml's quality-gate and other-platforms.yml's linux and macos, and

  running `cargo test` or `cargo clippy` whose effective `contents` permission is not `read`,
  and a checkout that keeps its credentials where the workflow grants write. At the red it
  named release.yml's quality-gate twice, once for each; at the green, nothing. Its companion,
  `test_the_token_reading_can_see_a_check_that_could_push`, sees an inherited write token, a
  job-level read that overrides it, a kept checkout, a clippy job with no permissions anywhere,
  and leaves alone a job that runs no check.
- **Sound (ledger 616).** `test_every_job_that_tests_on_windows_turns_the_sound_off` names each
  job running `cargo test` on a Windows runner with neither the workflow's nor its own env
  setting `WIXEN_NO_AUDIO`. At the red it named release.yml's quality-gate alone. Its companion,
  `test_the_sound_reading_can_see_a_workflow_missing_the_setting`, sees the missing setting,
  passes it at either level, leaves a clippy job and an Ubuntu job alone, and does not read a
  commented-out line as in force.
- **The workflows.** `release.yml`: `WIXEN_NO_AUDIO: "1"` in its env with ci.yml's comment
  copied; quality-gate with `permissions: contents: read`, `fetch-depth: 0` and
  `persist-credentials: false`, each with a comment. `other-platforms.yml`: `fetch-depth: 0`
  on both checkouts.
- **Counts.** `cargo test --test house_style` 78 passed, 74 on `main`;
