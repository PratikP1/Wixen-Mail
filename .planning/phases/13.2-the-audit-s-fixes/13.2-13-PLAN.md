<objective>
Keep two things a stranger's message can make Wixen Mail do from
happening: the reply editor fetching the sender's pictures, and a forged
meeting update moving the calendar.

Purpose: Pratik's request of 2026-10-14 carries R5 of the audit, finding
#19, and the unreported finding #21. Reading a newsletter fetches no
tracking pixel, by design; pressing Ctrl+R on it does, because the
editor's page runs only the cleaner and never the reader's hold-back
step. And a meeting update forging the organiser's From moves the
meeting and queues the move for Google or Microsoft even when the
receiving server stamped it dmarc=fail and the list shows it as
Phishing, because the decision reads only the From address.
