<objective>
When the settings file cannot be read, keep it, tell the person once, and make sure nothing
writes over it, so a disk fault or a hand edit stops being a silent reset.

Purpose: ledger 889, left by 13.2-23. Since 13.2-23 a save can no longer leave the settings
half written, but a disk fault or a hand edit still can, and then every reading of the
settings fails: the person meets the defaults with no sentence, Allow Changes answers nothing
for every account, and OK in Settings writes the defaults over the file. Pratik answered yes
to a plan for it on 2026-10-17/18.

Output: the keeping in the data layer, the start-up step and its sentence, Settings refusing
rather than overwriting, the cases, the changelog, ledger 889 closed and a row for the ear,
the three marks, and the pull request.
