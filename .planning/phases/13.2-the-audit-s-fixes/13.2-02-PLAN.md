<objective>
Make a change made here show on its conversation row at once, and land the
cursor after a conversation row leaves, so the list says what the
announcement already said.

Purpose: Pratik, 2026-10-14: "Read/unread status change doesn't apply
immediately. While the announcement says it does, the change only takes
place when message sync occurs. This is confusing." And: "Delete is the
same as 2." One cause: under conversation view the list paints from
`s.conversations`, a snapshot only a full re-read replaces, and every path
that changes a message here writes `s.messages`, the store and the server,
and never that snapshot. A full re-read is not the fix: its arm announces
the mailbox count whatever the reason, it does nothing under All Inboxes,
and at the size his 2026-10-06 log recorded it re-reads 22,503
conversations on every key press.

Output: the narrowed listing, the pure rule, the catch-up and its call
sites, the cases and the reading, the timing row, the changelog, the ledger,
the four marks, and the pull request.
