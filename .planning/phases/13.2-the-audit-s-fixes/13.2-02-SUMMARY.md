# Phase 13.2 Plan 02: A change made here shows on its conversation row Summary

Under conversation view, a mark, a star, a label, an undo of a mark, the automatic mark after
reading and a refused change put back now change their conversation row at once, read from the
store for the conversations they touched, and Mark as Read then offers Mark as Unread. A delete
or a move that empties a conversation in the folder takes its row out at once and lands the
cursor on the next conversation, or the one before it when it was the last, with the focus
event raised. Pratik's second and third reports of 2026-10-14.

## What was done

- **Task 1.** `MessageCache::conversations_holding` names the conversations of a set of
  messages with their accounts, a message deleted here included. The folder's and every
  inbox's conversation listings take a `Listing` (`Whole` or `OnlyNamed`), the named pairs
  bound as one JSON array read through `json_each`, never written into the statement. The pure
  rule `view_state::bring_rows_up_to_date` replaces a touched row in place, names the rows
  whose conversation came back empty, adds nothing, and repaints through `which_rows_changed`.
  `WhoseConversation` matches a row by account and conversation together.
- **Task 2.** `bring_the_conversation_rows_up_to_date` reads the conversations a change
  touched in the scope on screen (the folder, or every inbox under All Inboxes) with the reach
  and sort the loaders read (`the_conversation_reach`, extracted from both loaders), applies
  the rule, repaints only changed rows through `repaint_the_rows_that_changed` (extracted from
  the ConversationsLoaded arm's `MailArrived` branch, which now calls it), lands the cursor
  when rows left, and says nothing. It is called by `mark_these_read`, `star_these`,
  `label_the_message`, `run_these_actions_over` after its labels and its said-first phrase,
  `take_back_or_do_again` after its marks, `take_row_out_of_the_list`, the refusal that ends a
  set's wait, and the `MessageReadToggled`, `MessageStarredToggled` and `LabelsChanged` arms.
  `take_row_out_of_the_list` no longer returns under conversation view; `ASetLeaving`
  remembers the set's messages, and the Delete arm and `remember_these_rows_leaving` remember
  a set under conversation view through one `the_set_leaving`. The two comments of premise 6
  are corrected by dating.
- **Task 3.** The changelog's Fixed entry, ledger 834 to 838 and the note on 544, LIST-04's and
  LIST-21's dated lines and traceability rows, the four marks, the timing rows, and the pull
  request.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1] The narrowing moved into the scope.** The plan narrowed after the scope; at
   Pratik's size that read one conversation in 620 ms. As the plan directs above 100 ms,
   `elsewhere` and `here` are narrowed too, through `the_scope_over(scope, listing)` with a
   `named_at` placeholder on `AScope` (no third scope, no second column list, no copied scope
   line); `a_conversation_listing` lost its narrowing parameter, and `every_inbox_scope`, which
   nothing called after that, was deleted with its words moved to `EVERY_INBOX`. Two anchors
   moved with it and were rewritten and measured.
2. **[Rule 1] The landing case's fixture.** As first written, A was the newest conversation,
   so its row was row 0 and the case passed red: the cursor never moved and still read row 0.
   A is now the oldest, the last row, where only a landing puts the cursor on the row before.
   Observation 1009.
3. **[Shape] The frame is shown on the test's own desktop**, where nobody sees it, as
   `tests/deleting_a_message_lands_on_the_next_one.rs` shows its frame, rather than never
   shown as the plan said, so the list raises its focus event.
4. **[Scope] More callers than the plan listed:** the runner's said-first phrase, which the
   conversation row shows as its said-first column, and the refusal that ends a set's wait,
   which now catches up with the messages that did leave.
5. **[Shape] `WhoseConversation` in `src/application/conversations.rs`,** a file the plan did
   not list, beside `ConversationItem` and `ReadIn`.
6. **[Order] Task 1's green was committed after task 2's red was written.** The commit gate
   tests the working tree, so task 2's edits were set aside as a patch, three files restored
   with `git checkout`, task 1's green committed, and the patch applied again with `git
   apply`. Observation 1010.
7. **[Shape] The two update arms that remove a row** call `take_row_out_of_the_list` only when
   the store is open, since it now takes the store; with no store the list holds no rows.
8. **[Rule 2] A third timing step, `b533b1b3`.** Narrowing `elsewhere` left the read at 385
   ms, so a condition on the conversation alone was added for the thread index, a change
   with no new behaviour, held by the cases that compare the narrowed rows with the whole
   listing's; 299 ms. The plan named only the `elsewhere` step; the remaining distance to 100
   ms is ledger 837.
