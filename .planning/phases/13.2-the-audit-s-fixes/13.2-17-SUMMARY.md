# Phase 13.2 Plan 17: Google's expired contacts marker, and contacts typed here Summary

Google contacts keep syncing after the marker expires, read from Google's documented 400 naming
`EXPIRED_SYNC_TOKEN`, and a contact typed here goes to Google and Outlook on every sync rather
than only on a full read.

## What works now

- **Finding #25, fixed.** `is_expired_sync_token` is true for a 410 from either provider, and
  for a 400 whose message holds `EXPIRED_SYNC_TOKEN` or "Sync token is expired", ignoring case;
  false for any other 400 (Google's etag refusal among them), a 500 and a network error. With a
  marker stored and Google refusing it with its own body, `sync_google_contacts` reads the whole
  address book and stores the fresh marker. Google's page for `people.connections.list`, last
  updated 2024-08-06 and read 2026-10-16: "Sync tokens expire 7 days after the full sync. A
  request with an expired sync token will get an error with an google.rpc.ErrorInfo with reason
  "EXPIRED_SYNC_TOKEN"." Nobody has seen the answer from a live account (ledger 873).
- **Finding #26, fixed.** Both create blocks lost their full-read guard. A contact typed here
  after the first sync is created at Google and at Outlook on the next sync, and one Outlook
  refused on its first sync is sent again on the next. A contact an address book already knows,
  or one that came from the other address book, is not sent as new by either sync, held by two
  pins with markers stored.
- Reached in the running program through `spawn_contacts_sync` in `wx_app.rs`, which calls both
  syncs.
- REAL-01 gains a dated line and no `[D]` clause; its box stays open. The Outlook half of #26
  sits under no requirement: none names Outlook's contacts.
- `cargo test --lib application::contacts_sync::`: 287 passed, 281 before plus six.
