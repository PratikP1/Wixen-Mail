<objective>
Make the fold of a calendar's changed days find, change and delete rows only in the calendar
being read, for a subscribed feed and for a calendar server alike, so a feed naming a meeting
held in another calendar of the account leaves that meeting as it was.

Purpose: a security review of 2026-10-16 flagged 13.2-19's green commit, `e5d8516d`. It sends
each day a feed changed out of a series through `one_caldav_day_kept_out_of_its_series`, and
that function finds the series and the day's own row by the account and the event's name, and
deletes by the same, never asking which calendar a row is in. A feed is anyone's to publish.
One whose changed day carries the UID of a meeting in another calendar of the account can
move it, take it over or call a day of it off. The UID is not a secret: somebody who sent you
an invitation knows it, and if you then subscribe to their feed they can cancel or move that
meeting on your calendar. The calendar server's read calls the same function the same way, so
two server calendars carrying one meeting reach each other's copy with nobody hostile at all.

Output: the store's one-calendar lookup and delete, the fold narrowed for both callers, seven
cases red first, the changelog, a ledger row opened and fixed, the four marks. No pull request,
since no sentence, window or control changes.
</objective>
