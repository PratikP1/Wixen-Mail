# Phase 13.2 Plan 31.1: a press reads one conversation Summary

M or Delete on a conversation row now reads only that conversation: at the size of Pratik's
Inbox, the store work of M went from 484 ms to 20.5 ms on the store as written, and from 465
ms to 15.3 ms after a close and an open. Ledger 837 is fixed.

`idx_messages_folder_id` once under the listing and again under each correlated column, and
`elsewhere` searched by folder too. The rule over the rows on screen is now the largest step
left, 15 to 20 ms; it was not this plan's subject.

**The 100 ms budget is held only by the ignored case, run by hand. Nothing on a commit checks
the number again.** What runs on every commit is
`test_a_change_made_here_reads_only_its_conversations_through_the_thread_index` in
`storage_shape`, which holds how the reads search and not how long they take.

## What changed

- `src/data/message_cache/messages.rs`: `AScope::listed` is a function of the listing, so a
  narrowed listing asks the folder (or every inbox) for the named conversations alone; every
  folder term of the narrowed scope carries SQLite's unary plus through
  `Listing::off_the_folder_index`; `narrowed_by` reuses the new
  `narrowed_to_the_named_threads`; `messages_in_one_conversation` reads
  `the_scope_over(&ONE_FOLDER, Listing::OnlyNamed)` with its one pair bound through
  `as_bound_pairs`. The whole listing's text is byte for byte what it was. The scope readings
  and docs corrected by dating.
- `src/data/message_cache/mod.rs`: the reading and its pure helper
  `reads_messages_only_by_conversation`, with two planted companions.
- The row-and-action reading now also compares each narrowed row with the whole listing's row,
  over every fixture, folder, reach and conversation; green on `main` and after.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Shape] Every inbox's `listed` is narrowed by the conversation alone.** Premise 5 narrowed
   it "by the pairs bound at `?2`". The set is keyed by account and conversation, and the row's
   own pair is looked up in it, while `here` is already narrowed by the pair; the account half
   would add nothing. Both listings go through `narrowed_to_the_named_threads`, so the
   narrowing is still written once.
2. **[Shape] `AScope::listed` is a function, `fn(Listing, &str) -> String`,** named
   `listed_in_one_folder` and `listed_in_every_inbox`, which the plan left open.
3. **[Acceptance] The green run's control is partly outside the red run's spread.** The red
   run read 1.37 s and 1.80 s; the green 1.31 s, inside, and 1.15 s after the reopen, 0.22 s
   below. The machine was quieter; the press fell by a factor of about thirty over the same
   half, far outside that. Said on the measurements page.
4. **[Process] The green commit's hook refused once.** The planning edits for task 3 were
   already in the working tree, and `the_planning_files_agree_with_themselves` read them
   (a count of 292 against 291 summaries on disk, and the ledger's table edited without its
   JSON half). They were set aside as a patch with `git diff` and `git checkout`, the green
   committed, and the patch put back with `git apply`, as 13.2-02's deviation 6 did; the JSON
   halves of 834 and 837 were then written.
5. **[Records] The narrowing record's library half is named in its comment** rather than as a
   second record (observation 935 allows either), and the rewritten record now names the
   reading as well, since a hand-written query searches by folder.
6. **[Process] The re-measure call named 19 records,** the 14 flagged and the five written or
   rewritten, so each new record has the tool's verdict as well as the hand measurement.
7. **[Type] The green commit is typed `perf`, not `feat`.** It changes how long the press takes
   and nothing it answers, and the row-and-action reading holds the answers equal.
