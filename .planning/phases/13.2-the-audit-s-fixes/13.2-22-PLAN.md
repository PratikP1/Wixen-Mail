<objective>
Stop announcements made close together from overwriting each other. Every
line ends as one write to one hidden control plus one live-region event, and
NVDA reads the control's text when it handles the event, so two writes a few
microseconds apart are heard as the last one. Join what is released together
into one line, most urgent first, and hold the flush while the UI tick
drains its updates and while one command runs.

Purpose: audit finding #32 (R10 in the report), verdict "must". The
product's main channel drops a line whenever two things happen at once, and
the line dropped is usually the more important first one: a send failure's
reason, then "Disconnected", then "The Outbox is done", and only the last is
spoken. Pratik's request of 2026-10-14 is every item that needs fixing.

Output: the join, the hold, the two wired turns, the visual channel joined,
the cases, the guard records, the changelog, the ledger, MAIL-05's dated
line, the four marks, and a pull request so NVDA's job runs.
</objective>
