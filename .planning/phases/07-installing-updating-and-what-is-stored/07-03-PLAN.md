does: the status is derived at `ScreenReaderBridge::default`, every call into

not give. The derivation is `cfg!(target_os = "windows")`, which is a hardcoded
platform list of one, so writing a macOS bridge would leave the warning
appearing. And the status covers announcements only, while the other half of the
bridge, the accessible names, has no marker anywhere.

answered by which bridge module compiled, said in the About dialog and at
