<objective>
Say the two things this project has decided and not said where a person meets
them: that the downloaded mail is not encrypted, and that signing will not make
the Windows warning disappear.

Purpose: success criterion 4 in full, and the half of criterion 1 that is not
blocked on a certificate. Criterion 4 asks that the cache-is-not-encrypted
decision be said "once and clearly where a user meets it: the first-run screen
and the page about what is stored". The page has it. The first-run screen has
nothing about storage at all. Criterion 1's last clause says the SmartScreen
outcome is stated and not promised, and the promise that was in
`docs/installing.md` has already been taken out by hand, which is exactly the
condition under which it comes back.

Output: a first-run screen and a `--help` that say what is on the disk, four
documents that agree about signing, a guard that stops the signing promise
returning, and a guard that stops a path being added to the data folder without
being written down.
</objective>
