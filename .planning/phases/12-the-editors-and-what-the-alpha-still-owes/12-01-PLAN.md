**2. The two likeliest causes, and the read that tells them apart.**

- **The page window never came back to the front.** `activateWindow`
  runs `WScript.Shell.AppActivate(title)` from a PowerShell process that
  is not the foreground process; Windows' foreground lock lets such a
  call find the window and flash its taskbar button rather than bring it
  forward, and `AppActivate` answers true either way. Then `K` went to
  Edge, whose first-run window had focus in its address bar, where a
  typed letter is spoken only with NVDA's speak-typed-characters on,
  which the disposable NVDA's configuration does not promise; an empty
  phrase is what that looks like in Guidepup's log. What fits: no focus
  announcement after the activation, though the log holds one after the
  browser opened ("document"), and the harness's README says the log
  never holds a window's opening speech but does hold what a key made
  NVDA say.
- **The page window came back and the document did not take focus.**
  wxWidgets restores a re-activated frame's focus to its last focused
  child, the WebView, and `wxWebViewEdge::OnSetFocus`
  (`target/debug/wxWidgets/src/msw/webview_edge.cpp:1171-1175`) calls
  `MoveFocus(PROGRAMMATIC)` on the controller, which is the chain that
  should put focus in the document; if it does not hold on this tree,
  browse mode has no document and `K` is not a next-link key. This is
  the product-side cause and it is excluded by a measurement, not by the
  argument above.
