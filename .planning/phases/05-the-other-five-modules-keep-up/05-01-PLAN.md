<objective>
Settle by running it whether a moved occurrence is already shown once, and then
make a day that was changed on its own say so when it is read.

Purpose: criterion 3 of phase 5, second half. `05-RESEARCH.md` assumption A1
says a moved occurrence appears twice, once expanded from the series and once
as its own row, and says plainly that this came from reading rather than
running and should be the first failing test of any PIM-03 plan. Reading the
same code again on 2026-09-06 says the opposite, and gives a reason: every path
that stores a moved day also takes that day off the series. Neither reading is
evidence. A test is.

What is missing either way is the second clause of PIM-03's second `[D]` line:
an exception "announces that it differs from the series". Nothing on the display
row carries the fact and nothing said out loud mentions it.

Output: one integration test that settles the de-duplication and would notice if
it broke, and a row that says when it is a day changed on its own.
</objective>
