<objective>
Wire the download of everything into the window: started by every check,
covering every enabled IMAP account, chunk by chunk, headers then text, the
folder on screen first, with a Pause on the Tools menu, a wait after a
refusal served by the main timer, and the two experimental commands it
replaces retired.

downloaded per folder. All email should be downloaded." And #23: "Unless
explicitly forbidden, message text should be downloaded along with mail." The
model is 10-01's, the list is unbounded since 10-02, the text stays since
10-03, and what is spoken is 10-04's. What is left is the worker, the
commands, and the documents that name them.

Output: the runner reached from every check, the Pause, the retirements, one
target rewritten with its record, the shortcuts page and the changelog.
Closes #20 and #23 on the tree side; whether a provider tolerates it is the
tester's account's to answer.
