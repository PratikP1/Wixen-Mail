<objective>
Keep mail arriving on its own: restart the inbox watch with a growing wait,
try again the moment the network returns, give every enabled IMAP account a
watch, check on a schedule where a watch cannot cover (POP, a server without
IDLE, a watch that keeps failing, the other kept folders), check once at
startup, and make the status line say what is true.

Purpose: #37 in the tester's words: "After running a few hours, automatic
mail fetching is switched off. The user has to manually fetch mail." The
cause is a decision: the one watch ends and nothing restarts it, by design
and with a comment saying so. Two things the issue did not say: the watch is
never started at all until the first F9, because only a finished check
starts one, and the account editor has offered a Check Interval since it was
written that nothing reads.

Output: the watch that keeps going, the schedule, the startup check, the
status line, the interval field made true, one target with its record, the
cold-start rows re-taken, and the changelog entry. Closes #37 on the tree
side; whether Gmail drops IDLE and how the restart behaves over hours is the
tester's account's to answer.
</objective>


So the 29-minute window is not what ends a watch; a server closing the
connection, the network going, or DONE failing on a dead socket is. A dead
