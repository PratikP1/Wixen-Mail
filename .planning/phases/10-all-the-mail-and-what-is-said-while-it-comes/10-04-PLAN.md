<objective>
Separate the two kinds of line a fetch produces at the source, make how much
of it is spoken a setting a person chooses, and raise the answer to a
keystroke above the run of progress so it is heard.

Purpose: #38 in the tester's words: "When fetching mail and other items, the
announcements are too verbose. Only folders and items with new mail or items
should be announced," and, added the same day: "Or make this user-configurable.
Let the user decide how much to announce while fetching items." Every status
line is spoken today at Low under one topic, which coalesces only when lines
arrive faster than they are spoken, and a check of fifty folders speaks fifty
lines. "Settings saved" rides the same channel and is replaced by the next
sync line before it is heard.

Output: the setting end to end, two new update kinds with their arms, every
sync path's lines sorted into one kind, a reading that holds them there, and
the changelog entry. Closes #38 on the tree side; which is which by ear is a
listening pass.
</objective>
