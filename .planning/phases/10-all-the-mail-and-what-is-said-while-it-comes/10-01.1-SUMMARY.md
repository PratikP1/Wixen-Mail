# Phase 10 Plan 01.1: Every Settings check box stays a check box, and a page reached from inside a page hands focus to its first control Summary

**The six Settings pages after General are painted at the end of their own build, after their
controls exist, so no checkbox is created under a painted panel and none is made owner-drawn:
measured over MSAA, the channel NVDA reads for a `Button`-class window, every one of the 20
later-page check boxes answers `ROLE_SYSTEM_CHECKBUTTON` and toggles on a click, in the default
and dark themes, where the tree before this plan had all 20 per theme answering push button with
a state that never moved (#67). And a page reached from inside another page, the way Ctrl+Tab
reaches it once wxWidgets has read the key, puts native focus on its first control rather than on
the empty panel, while an arrow on the tab row still leaves focus on the row (#68). Each fix is
held by a reading that builds the real dialog and by a guard record measured by hand, so a commit
to `wx_settings.rs` runs both. Both issues closed from the merge commit `d020aa60` with what the
readings proved and what only the tester's ear settles.** Nothing pushed.
