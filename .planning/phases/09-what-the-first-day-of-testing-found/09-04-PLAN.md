wxWidgets makes consecutive radio items one group and ends the group at a

issue's `:14165` comment worried about. The separators were there for reading;
what a screen reader gains from them is a pause between four pairs, and what
it loses is a menu whose ticks lie, so they go. Adding "Then by" to the menu
is not asked for and is not done.
