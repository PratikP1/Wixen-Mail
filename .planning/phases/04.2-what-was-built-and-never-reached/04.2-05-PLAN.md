<objective>
Say the sentence this program already writes about held-back pictures.

Purpose: someone reading a marketing message with a screen reader meets
`[Picture not shown: ...]` thirty times, and is never told there were thirty,
never told why in one sentence, and never told where the switch is. The inline
markers carry the fact. The orientation and the way out are the part that is
missing, and both were written and are unreachable. This is the module the
project's own principles use as its example of a privacy decision worth
explaining.

Output: the count kept where it is produced, the sentence placed in the document
in reading order, and the composer's own preview left alone.
</objective>

leaves the next reader wondering whether the other was considered.
