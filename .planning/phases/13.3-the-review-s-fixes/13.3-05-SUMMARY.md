# Phase 13.3 Plan 05: Reply and Forward say the text is not here Summary

From an opened conversation, Reply, Reply All, Reply to Sender Only and Forward on a message
whose text is not on this computer now open no compose window. They say so, and Wixen Mail
starts downloading the text, so the same key works a moment later; offline they say the text
cannot be downloaded until Wixen Mail goes back online, and start nothing (review item 4).
Forward no longer works out a reply address, and `MessageItem::reply_address` is gone with
its three tests (review item 37). A message whose text is here quotes exactly as before.

## What changed

- `src/application/reply.rs`: `WhatQuotingFinds` (the text, not here yet, not here while
  offline), `fetches()`, `refusal()`, `what_quoting_finds(stored, reachability)`, and the two
  sentences. A stored row holding neither half reads as no text, as the list's cursor reads
  it. Five cases.
- `src/presentation/wx_app.rs`: `the_body_a_command_quotes` takes the message and answers
  `TheQuote::Here` or `TheQuote::NotHere`, asking the decision with `reachability_of`;
  `the_text_is_not_here_yet` starts `spawn_body_fetch` when the decision says to and signals
  `ActionRefused` with its sentence; `start_reply` and the `ID_FORWARD` arm call it before
  any `open_compose`. With nothing named the preview is still the list's source.
- `msg_info` answers the subject and the quote; the Forward arm reads `(subj, quote)`.
- `src/presentation/ui_types.rs`: `reply_address`, its three tests and `addressed` removed;
  79 tests to 76.
- `tests/the_conversation_window_acts_on_the_message_under_its_cursor.rs`: the reading
  `test_reply_and_forward_say_the_text_is_not_here_and_fetch_it` and its planted companion
  `test_the_quote_reading_complains_at_each_part_left_out`, six plants; 27 tests to 29.
