# Phase 13.3 Plan 24.5: A Google permission left unticked is said with its button Summary

A Google calendar, contacts or tasks sync that Google refuses 403 because the sign-in was not
given that permission now says which of the three Google would not give, names the one button
in the Account Manager that makes this account's sign-in again, and plays the cue for an
account needing attention, instead of counting one error. The calendar sync stops at a list
refused that way. Every other refusal is counted as before.

## What works now

- `asked_and_answered::names_a_permission_not_given` reads Google's error object and answers
  yes for `insufficientPermissions` in `error.errors[].reason` or
  `ACCESS_TOKEN_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT` in `error.details[].reason`, compared exactly.
  `a_permission_not_given()` is `Error::Api` 403 from google carrying the word
  `insufficientPermissions` and no body; `is_a_permission_not_given` matches it alone.
- `GoogleApiClient::read_the_body` (calendar list, contacts) and `TasksClient`'s read, for a
  Google address only, return that error for a 403 the reader names. Every other tasks refusal
  keeps "The task service refused the request: {status}".
- `every_google_calendar::sync` returns that error at the list, before any calendar is read.
- `who_holds_the_calendars::why_google_gave_nothing` turns it into `TheBrowserSignInLacksThem`
  or `TheSeparateSignInLacksThem`; the Google pass of `spawn_calendar_sync`,
  `spawn_contacts_sync` and `spawn_tasks_sync` sets `why_not_asked` from it and counts the
  error as before when it answers nothing. `git grep -c "why_google_gave_nothing(" --
  src/presentation/wx_app.rs` answers 3, one in each spawner.
- Help's "If it does not work" has a bullet for the sentence, and "Calendars arrive and
  contacts or tasks do not" names the right button by account instead of `Alt+T` alone.
- Test counts at the end, from the source: `service::asked_and_answered` 17 (15 at `main` on
  2026-10-22), `service::tasks_api` 63 (62), `application::who_holds_the_calendars` 49 (45),
  `application::every_google_calendar` 16 (15 after 13.3-17), `service::google_api` unchanged
  at 41.
