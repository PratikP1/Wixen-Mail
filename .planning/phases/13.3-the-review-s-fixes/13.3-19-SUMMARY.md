# Phase 13.3 Plan 19: Hidden blocks dropped from markup of any depth Summary

What a sender hid is now taken out of markup nested past 512 levels exactly as out of any other
markup, before the formatted view and `long_text`'s reader see it, because the walk that finds
hidden blocks is one loop over `traverse()` and the depth bypass 13.2-12 added is gone (review
item 27).

## What works now

- A message nested 513 levels deep with a `display:none` preheader padded with filler and an
  `aria-hidden` decoration: the page holds neither the preheader, the filler nor the
  decoration, and says nothing about blocks left out. A hidden block of words below visible
  text at the same depth is left out and the one-block sentence is said once, above the first
  word.
- `pieces_of_markup` over the same message gives words with no preheader, filler or
  decoration in them.
- `drop_what_the_sender_hid` over 4,000 nested `div` elements on a 1 MiB thread finishes with
  the hidden block gone and a count of 1, run in a process of its own.
- Every case that existed before in `application::hidden_text` and in the target passes
  unchanged, apart from the retired bypass case.
