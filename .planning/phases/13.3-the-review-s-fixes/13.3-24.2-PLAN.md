<objective>
Make Reply and Forward, and the message list's own landing, tell a message whose text has not
arrived from one that came down with no text at all; make Reply and Forward from the message list
ask the store the same question an opened conversation asks; and make Forward with no message
chosen refuse as Reply does.

Purpose: ledgers 919, 917 and 918, opened by 13.3-05 on 2026-10-21 as `todo` rows for Pratik to
schedule, and his "yes to 3" of 2026-10-22, which brings all three into phase 13.3 before its
close. Since 13.3-05 an encrypted message, or one that is only an attachment, is refused at
every press of Reply or Forward in a conversation with a promise that its text is downloading,
and the list downloads it again at every landing, while its text never comes because it has
none. From the list, Reply or Forward pressed before a message's text arrives quotes the
placeholder "Downloading this message...", and Forward with no message chosen opens an empty
message where Reply refuses.

Output: the store's typed reading, the decision taking it, the list path reading the store, the
one refusal for no message, the readings that hold the wiring, the changelog, the ledger, the
four marks, and the pull request.
</objective>
