# Phase 13.3 Plan 24.3: A refused account save says and holds what is kept Summary

After a refused account save the main window, and the Account Manager opened again, hold the
accounts the store holds; a new account whose save is refused leaves no password, directory
password or browser sign-in behind; and a removal Windows will not let go of is said as
"Work was not removed, because Windows would not let go of its saved password; try again, and
see When something goes wrong in Help if it keeps happening.", the store's reasons in the log.

## What changed

- `src/data/message_cache/accounts.rs`: `replace_accounts_and_read_back(given, before)`
  answering `AccountsAfterTheSave { held, refused }`; `replace_accounts` keeps the list it
  loads and saves through `save_or_forget_a_new_account`, which forgets every secret of a new
  account whose save is refused and logs what would not go; `delete_account(account_id,
  called)` calls `forget_every_secret_of`, logs each reason with `tracing::warn!` by the
  account's name and answers `Error::InPlainWords(not_removed(called, &kept))`. `KeptSecret`
  orders and names the three kinds; `whose` gives "This account" for a blank name. Eleven new
  cases: four for the read-back, three for the forgetting (one of them the refused edit that
  keeps its secrets), one for the plain sentence with the log, three on the sentence alone.
  The 13.3-07 case now wants "Work was not removed".
- `src/data/message_cache/mod.rs`: re-exports `AccountsAfterTheSave`.
- `src/presentation/wx_app.rs`: `handle_account_mgr` calls
  `cache.replace_accounts_and_read_back(new, &accounts)` first, outside the lock, announces a
  refusal as before, and works out `removed`, `added`, `stopping`, the active account, the
  default (through `default_after_change`, persisted when it moved) and `s.accounts` from the
  accounts held.
- `tests/mail_keeps_arriving_on_its_own.rs`: `what_is_wrong_with_what_it_works_from`, its case
  over the handler, and a companion over three written handlers (today's, one that works out
  the first syncs before the save, and the shape written here).
- Test call sites of `delete_account` in `identities.rs`, `quick_steps.rs` and
  `saved_searches.rs` pass a name.
