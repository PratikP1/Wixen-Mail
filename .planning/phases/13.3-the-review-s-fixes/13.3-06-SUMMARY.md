# Phase 13.3 Plan 06: The open folder's re-read is applied in the tick that reads it Summary

While the download of everything brings a folder you have open, the main timer reads that
folder again at most every ten seconds. It now does so before the tick drains the workers'
updates, so the rows it reads are applied in the same tick, before any key is handled. Before,
it ran after the drain, its rows waited 50 ms for the next tick, and a Delete or an M handled
in between was written over: the row came back with its old Inbox number, or turned unread
again, and a second Delete on a row that came back went off the server instead of to Trash
(review item 5).

## What changed

- `src/presentation/wx_app.rs`: the due block, `let due = lock_state(&state)` through the
  `reread_folder_if_open` call, moved unchanged from after the held mail to directly above the
  comment that opens the drain. Its comment gains why it sits there. Nothing else in the tick
  moved. `git grep -n` reads `.the_folder_due(std::time::Instant::now());` at `:6651` and
  `while let Ok(update) = ui_rx.try_recv()` at `:6668`.
- `tests/everything_comes_down_without_being_asked.rs`: `what_is_wrong_with_the_timers_reread`
  reads the whole tick, from `"First timer tick, event loop is running"` to
  `"let somewhere_to_type = ["`, says so when the tick has no drain there, and complains when
  `ui_rx.try_recv()` comes before `.the_folder_due(`. Its companion
  `test_the_reading_would_see_the_timer_leave_the_folder_unread` plants three timers, each
  answered by exactly one complaint: no due block, a re-read for an arrival, and the order
  13.2-09 left. No test function added or removed.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3] The new record's `before` runs to the drain's closing `});`,** not to its
   `while let` line as the plan wrote. A swap that stops at the `while let` line cannot put the
   due block after the drain and still compile, and a break that does not compile reddens every
   test. The longer `before` still names one place.
2. **[Plan] The reading also says when the tick has no drain where it reads,** so a renamed
   channel cannot leave the order unread and the reading quiet.
