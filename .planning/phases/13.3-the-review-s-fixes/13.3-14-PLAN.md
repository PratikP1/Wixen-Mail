whole list or the whole account.

every row on screen, on the interface thread under the state lock, so a mark over thousands of
conversations in a large folder freezes the window and the screen reader for seconds. Item 32
(nice): `the_set_leaving` works out each message's place in the loaded list and throws it away
under conversation view. Item 33 (nice): landing on a conversation row lists its missing text
over the whole account's scope, the shape 13.2-31.1 measured at 138 to 146 ms and replaced
everywhere else. Pratik answered yes to all three on 2026-10-20.
