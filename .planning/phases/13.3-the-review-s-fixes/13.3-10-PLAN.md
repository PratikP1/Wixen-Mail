<objective>

address book deleted while a change waited here stops failing on every sync.

Purpose: since 13.2-21 a whole read takes off what the answer does not name. Item 12 (must):
a block with no card text is skipped without being counted, so a server that cuts its answer
short (RFC 6352 section 8.6, status 507) or answers a card's text with 404 looks complete, and
the contacts it left out are taken off here. Item 24 (should): a contact deleted at the
address book and edited here is kept by the same pass, and every later sync sends the change,
is refused (404 at Google and Outlook, 412 at a CardDAV server) and counts one problem, for
good. Pratik answered yes to both on 2026-10-20.

Output: the reader's counts and the guard, the deleted-and-edited contact taken off and said,
the cases, the changelog, ledger 255's dated line, the guard records, the four marks.
