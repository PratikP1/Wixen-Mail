<objective>
Take the blocks a sender hid out of markup nested past 512 levels the same way as out of any
other markup, by making the walk that finds them iterative and deleting the depth bypass.

Purpose: item 27 of the review of 13.2's changes, graded should, 3 of 3. 13.2-12 made
`drop_what_the_sender_hid` hand markup nested deeper than `DEEPEST_MARKUP_WALKED` on unchanged,
on the premise that the page would hide what it hid. It cannot: the page's cleaner keeps
neither `style` nor `aria-hidden`, which is why the drop exists (#90). So a hostile or broken
message nested 513 levels deep has its hidden preheader, its padding and any `aria-hidden`
block shown in the formatted view and read by NVDA, with no sentence saying anything was left
out. Pratik said yes to fixing all 42 items on 2026-10-20.

Output: the iterative walk, the cases, the module doc, the guard records, the changelog, ledger
859 closed and the text reader's gap ledgered, the four marks, and the pull request.
