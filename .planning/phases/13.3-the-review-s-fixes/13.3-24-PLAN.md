<objective>
Make installing an update quit Wixen Mail the way Quit does, and make what the update code says
true.

Purpose: gaps 2 and 5 of `RESEARCH-the-update-mechanism.md`, under option (c) as 13.3-23's
checkpoint recorded Pratik's answer. Gap 2: the handover closes the window with
`frame.close(true)` and never tells the close handler this is a Quit, so with "keep running in
the notification area" on and the icon showing, the handler takes its hide branch: it says
"Wixen Mail is still running, in the notification area" a moment after saying Wixen Mail is
closing, and skips the Quit path's work. Nobody can meet it before an installer passes the
signer check, and no ledger row names it, which is why it is fixed now rather than found by the
first person it strands. Gap 5: the setting NVDA reads says the installer is about 12 MB, and it
is 15,725,012 bytes; several pages and comments describe the code before plan 07-09 landed.

comments and pages; the changelog, the ledger rows, the requirement line, the four marks and the
