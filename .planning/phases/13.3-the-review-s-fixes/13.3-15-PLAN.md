<objective>
Make both Google browser sign-ins check the account the token is really used for, and learn
who signed in through a permission Google's consent screen never lets a person untick.

Purpose: 13.2-15 added a check that refuses a Google sign-in made as somebody else. It
compares Google's answer with the account's address box, while mail signs in with the
Username box, so a Gmail account set up with a send-as address on another domain is refused
a sign-in that would have worked, and told to choose an address Google cannot offer (item
19). And it asks Google who signed in by reading the primary calendar, so a person who
unticks Calendar on Google's consent screen has the whole sign-in refused, mail included,
with a sentence pointing at the wrong cause (item 20). Both are regressions from 13.2-15.
Pratik answered yes to fixing both on 2026-10-20.

Output: the userinfo question and the two permissions, the Username asked for and compared,
the sentences, the cases and a reading, Help's setup page and the privacy page, the two
sitting sheets' consent steps, the changelog, ledger 869's dated line and a row for the ear,
the four marks, and the pull request.
