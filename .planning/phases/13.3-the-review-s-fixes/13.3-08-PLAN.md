<objective>
Make the download of everything leave newly arrived mail for the check, so the account's rules
run on it and it is said, without the download calling a folder stopped; and stop a filed or
replayed message with no Message-ID, or one the destination's substring search mismatches, from
taking another message's number.

Purpose: items 8 and 9 of the review of 13.2 (both must), the two corrections to 13.2-08, and
Pratik's "yes" of 2026-10-20 to fixing all 42. 13.2-08 made a check take every message above the
newest held so its rules see all of them, and the download, which shares `sync_folder`, takes the
same mail first with no rules; and 13.2-08 made every rule move settle its row through a search
that, for a blank identifier, matches every message in the folder.

Output: the download's selection and its count, the runner counting through it, the settle's two
guards, their cases and readings, the guard records, the changelog, the ledger, the four marks,
and the pull request.
</objective>
