# Phase 13.3 Plan 07: An account removed and added back on its address saves

`replace_accounts` now removes every account taken away before it saves the rest, and no
refusal stops the others, so an account removed and added back on its address in one sitting is
stored and the old one goes with its mail and its secrets (review item 6).

## What was done

- **Task 1, red then green.** Five cases in `src/data/message_cache/accounts.rs`: removed and
  added back on the same address; the same in other capitals; an edit taking the address of an
  account removed beside it; a refused removal answering with its own sentence, the other edit
  kept and no password kept for the account on its address; a refused save not throwing away
  the edit after it. All five red at `05272aa3` with the count check, accepted by the red gate.
  The green at `ebef4287`: `replace_accounts` collects each removal's outcome, then each save's,
  and `the_first_refusal` answers the first and logs any later one by the account's name, with
  no id or password. Its doc comment says why the removals come first and why nothing stops.
- **Task 2.** The changelog entry under `[Unreleased]`, `### Fixed`; ledger 921 to 924 in both
  halves with the header counts; MAIL-04's dated line and traceability row; the four marks.
