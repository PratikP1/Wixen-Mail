Apply the download's paced re-read of the open folder in the tick that reads it, by moving the
timer's due block above the tick's drain of the workers' updates.

Purpose: item 5 of the review of 13.2 (must), and Pratik's "yes" of 2026-10-20 to fixing all 42.

unread again, for ten seconds or until the folder is next opened, and a second Delete on a row
that came back deletes it outright.
