<objective>
Make the update check download only what it could run, say what a check at start found without
opening a window, run one check at a time, and give up on a stalled connection; and first, put
the update question to Pratik in plain words and record his answer.

Purpose: Pratik asked on 2026-10-20 to consider ship-shape for the update mechanism, and said
of it that day "It's a published crate." The research (`RESEARCH-the-update-mechanism.md`)
confirmed it is a real, carefully made crate, and recommends not adopting it: it cannot offer a
tester the next alpha or the final 1.0.0, it needs wxDragon moved from 0.9.17 to 0.9.22 or later,
and its windows were not built for a screen reader. It recommends keeping Wixen Mail's own update
code and fixing five gaps in it in this phase, with no package change. This plan opens with his
word on that (task 1) and then fixes gaps 1, 3 and 4. 13.3-24 fixes gaps 2 and 5.

Gap 1 matters most: once any newer release is published, a copy that chose a kind of version
downloads about 15.7 MB at every start, refuses it because nothing can pass the signer check
while the profile identifier is empty, and opens a dialog. Gap 3: two checks can write the same
file. Gap 4: neither client has a time limit, so a stalled connection holds the check for good.

Output: the checkpoint's answer; the decision, the guard, the limits and the folder rule with
their cases; the window's wiring; the guard records; the corrected pages; the changelog, the
ledger row, the requirement line, the four marks and the pull request.
