<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| a reading to a page | a page reworded, renamed or moved can turn a reading vacuous without a failure |
| a correction to a record | dating a narrative is a correction; changing its figure falsifies it |
| an external process to a test | `git rev-list` can fail, answer for the wrong repository, or answer nothing |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-08-06 | Denial of service | the provenance reading passing over a renamed directory or an emptied list | high | mitigate | it refuses a walk that read fewer than five files or found no figure of any kind; the walk list is held by an assertion on the precedent of `test_the_check_is_looking_at_the_whole_project`. |
| T-08-07 | Tampering | a narrative figure "corrected" to today's value | high | mitigate | task 1's action says a narrative gains a date and nothing else; the summary reports every named paragraph with its kind and the before and after line. |
