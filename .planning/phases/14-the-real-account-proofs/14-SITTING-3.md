# Sitting 3: deleting in Gmail, and undoing a delete

This sitting deletes proof messages in your Gmail account, online, offline
and across a restart, deletes one permanently, holds one back with the
account's own box, and undoes a delete before and after the server has heard
of it. About 25 to 35 minutes. It does not need your Google key.

The agent corrects this sheet against the build you installed and reads
your profile before you start. If a sentence you hear differs from the one
quoted here, the build is right and the sheet is wrong: say what you heard.

## What every sitting shares

- **Proof messages only.** Every message you send and delete today has a
  subject beginning "Wixen proof", so the agent may print it. Nothing else
  of yours is touched.
- **Nothing runs on this computer while you work.** The agent waits until
  you say you are done.
- **Turning the network off stops the agent too.** Several steps here take
  this computer offline with Airplane mode. You work the sheet alone and
  tell the agent when you are done.
- **Gmail on the web or on your phone is the other client.** A second
  desktop mail program on the same account can meet Gmail's limit on
  connections and look like a failure of what is being tested.
- **A browser sign-in lasts seven days while your key is in Testing.** This
  sitting does not use one.

## What you need

- The build installed, from `14-INSTALLING-THE-BUILD.md`.
- Gmail open on the web, for its settings at step 3, and on the web or your
  phone for the rest.

## Steps

1. Start Wixen Mail and let the first check finish.
2. Send yourself ten messages, however is quickest (Ctrl+Shift+M for a new message, Ctrl+Enter to send), with the subjects "Wixen proof delete 1" to "Wixen proof delete 5", "Wixen proof gone 1", "Wixen proof held 1", and "Wixen proof several 1" to "Wixen proof several 3".
3. Press F9, Check Mail, so the ten arrive in Inbox.
4. In Gmail on the web, open the settings, then the tab about forwarding and IMAP. Note, in Gmail's words, what the line about a message "marked as deleted and expunged from the last visible IMAP folder" is set to, and what the line about expunging at once is set to.
5. Delete online: on "Wixen proof delete 1", press Delete.
6. Delete offline and back: turn Airplane mode on.
7. On "Wixen proof delete 2", press Delete.
8. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
9. Delete offline across a restart: turn Airplane mode on.
10. On "Wixen proof delete 3", press Delete.
11. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
12. Turn Airplane mode off, start Wixen Mail, and let the check finish.
13. Delete permanently: on "Wixen proof gone 1", press Shift+Del, Delete Permanently. It is not asked about first, so no question should come. If one does, say what it said.
14. Hold a delete back: press Ctrl+Shift+A to open the Account Manager.
15. Choose the Gmail account in the list.
16. Press Alt+E, Edit.
17. Press Alt+N, Next, to the second page.
18. Press Alt+M to go to "Send and delete mail from this account", and press Space to untick it.
19. Choose OK.
20. Press Alt+C, Close.
21. On "Wixen proof held 1", press Delete. Say what was said.
22. Press F9. Say what was said.
23. In Gmail on your phone or the web, say where "Wixen proof held 1" is.
24. Press Ctrl+Shift+A, choose the Gmail account, and press Alt+E, Edit.
25. Press Alt+N, Next.
26. Press Alt+M and press Space to tick "Send and delete mail from this account" again.
27. Choose OK.
28. Press Alt+C, Close.
29. Press F9.
30. Undo before the server heard: turn Airplane mode on.
31. On "Wixen proof delete 4", press Delete.
32. Press Ctrl+Z.
33. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
34. Undo after the server heard: on "Wixen proof delete 5", press Delete.
35. Wait ten seconds.
36. The alpha page's item 20: open the Edit menu with Alt+E and listen to Undo's name. It should name the delete and the message, such as "Undo Delete: Wixen proof delete 5".
37. Press Escape.
38. Press Ctrl+Z. You should hear "Undid Delete on Wixen proof delete 5." Say where the cursor landed.
39. Listening item 65, its delete part: go to "Wixen proof several 1" and press Shift+Down twice.
40. Press Delete. You should hear "Delete" once, and then the row after the set read once when they have gone.
41. In Gmail, say where each proof message is: look in the Trash and in Inbox, and in All Mail for "Wixen proof gone 1".
42. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.

## At the end

Tell the agent:

- your setting from step 4, in Gmail's words;
- one line for each step that went differently from this sheet, in your
  words;
- "done".
