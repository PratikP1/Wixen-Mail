<objective>
Make each write path say what it did, in the log, in the shape the
sittings are read from: which message, what the server answered, and
where the message is now.

Purpose: the proofs of REAL-02 are decided by four readings, and one of
them is the log (PROPOSAL.md, "How a sitting works"). Today two copies
made in one minute cannot be told apart in it, a crossing that worked
writes nothing, a replay the server had already carried out writes
nothing, a sign-in does not say how it signed in, and a crash names no
build. Research 3 found all five (RESEARCH-3, "What the log cannot
answer"). This plan answers the first four. The fifth, the crash entry
naming its build, moved to 14-06 on 2026-10-05 after the plan check
found this plan at 17 files across three subsystems; 14-06 already
changes how the page window writes `crash.log`, so the two writers of
that file become one there (premise 4). The lines go in at Info,
because Pratik's profile stores `info` and keeps it (the proposal's
decisions the planner took).

Output: one line per replayed change within an account; four lines at
most per crossing; the sign-in kind on IMAP, POP and the send line;
their cases and readings; the guard records; the changelog; REAL-02's
dated line and the four marks.
</objective>
