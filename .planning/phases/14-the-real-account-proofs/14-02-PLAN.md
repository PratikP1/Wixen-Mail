`with_retry` (`google_api.rs:915-939`) sends a request again on 429, 5xx
or a network failure, up to three times, so one call can be four
requests and writes four lines. `tasks_api::refusal` (`:755-768`) drops
the body on purpose, because "a body from a failed request can carry the
token back"; Google's reason is read out of the body as one word and the
body itself is never kept.

**2. A request's address carries values that may not reach the log.**
`events_url` (`google_api.rs:505-531`) puts `syncToken`, `timeMin`,
`timeMax` and `pageToken` in the query, and `connections_url` (`:459-470`)
the same; a reqwest error's text names the whole address, query and
all, so the line is built from the parts and never from an error's text.
A calendar's identity can be an address (Google files a person's main
calendar under the account's own address, and a calendar shared by
somebody else under theirs: `calendarList` reference, read 2026-10-05),
and 14-04 puts such identities in the path, so a path segment holding
`@` or its encoded form `%40` is masked with
`common::logging::mask_email` (`src/common/logging.rs:163-179`).
