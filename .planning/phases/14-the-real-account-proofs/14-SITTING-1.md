# Sitting 1, done third: Google's calendars, contacts and tasks

**Waiting since 14 October.** This sitting waits until two things are done: the fixes from
the audit of 14 October and for the four problems you reported that day, which are phase
13.2, and a new build made after them, which you install first. The agent then corrects this
sheet against that build before you start, so the build and the sentences named below may
change.

The sittings run in the order 2, 3, 1, 4. This one needs your Google key,
and you said the key comes later, so it waits until you say the key is
placed. The new build has already started, in Sitting 2.

This sitting signs in to Google through the browser for your calendars,
contacts and tasks, and makes one event, one contact and one task on each
side to read back on the other. It is issue #22. About 40 to 60 minutes.

The agent corrects this sheet against the build you installed and reads
your profile before you start. If a sentence you hear differs from the one
quoted here, the build is right and the sheet is wrong: say what you heard.

## What every sitting shares

- **Proof items only.** Everything you make has a title beginning "Wixen
  proof", or for a contact the names "Wixen" and "Proof", so the agent may
  print it. Nothing else of yours is touched.
- **Nothing runs on this computer while you work.** The agent waits until
  you say you are done.
- **Turning the network off stops the agent too.** This sitting never needs
  it.
- **Gmail on the web or on your phone is the other client.** A second
  desktop mail program on the same account can meet Gmail's limit on
  connections and look like a failure of what is being tested.
- **A browser sign-in lasts seven days while your key is in Testing.** A
  refusal from day eight is Google's rule, not a fault.

## What you need

- The build installed, from `14-INSTALLING-THE-BUILD.md`, and Sittings 2
  and 3 done.
- Your Google key placed as `oauth.toml`.
- Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Tasks open on the web or your
  phone.

## Steps

1. Before anything else, read this: every item you make today is named "Wixen proof", or for the contact "Wixen" and "Proof", and nothing else of yours is touched.
2. Start Wixen Mail from the Start menu and let the first check finish. There is nothing to press.
3. Listening item 64: in the folder tree, go to Inbox, then press Tab into the message list. Listen for the newest message's row read once and not twice. Then press Shift+Tab back to the tree and Tab again: you should land on the row you left, with nothing moved.
4. Open Tools, Sync Calendar (Alt+T, then D), before any browser sign-in. You should hear, possibly after the counts: "Nothing was asked of Google for this account's calendars. The account signs in to mail with an app password, and Google gives calendars only to a browser sign-in. Open the Account Manager with Ctrl+Shift+A, choose the account and press Sign In for Calendars, Contacts and Tasks." If your key is not placed yet, you hear instead that this copy of Wixen Mail has no Google sign-in key; stop there and tell the agent.
5. Press Ctrl+Shift+A to open the Account Manager.
6. Choose your Gmail account in the list.
7. Press Alt+T, Sign In for Calendars, Contacts and Tasks. You should hear: "Signing in to Google for the account's calendars, contacts and tasks. Finish in the browser."
8. In the browser, sign in with your Google account. Google says it has not verified this app: that is expected for a key in Testing, and the app is your own project. Choose to continue.
9. Allow the three permissions Google lists: calendars, contacts and tasks.
10. Go back to Wixen Mail. You should hear: "Signed in to Google for this account's calendars, contacts and tasks. They are brought when you close the Account Manager."
11. Press Alt+C, Close. The three syncs run once by themselves. Say what you heard.
12. Open Tools, Sync Calendar. You should hear "Calendar sync:" and the counts, "N created, N updated, N deleted", with "N calendars read" added when more than one calendar was read.
13. Open Tools, Sync Contacts (Alt+T, then N). You should hear "Contacts sync:" and the counts.
14. Open Tools, Sync Tasks (Alt+T, then T). You should hear "Tasks:" and how many tasks in how many lists.
15. Tell the agent how many calendars your Google account has, so it can be held against what came.
16. In Google Calendar, on the web or your phone, make one event titled "Wixen proof Google event".
17. In Google Contacts, make one contact named "Wixen Proof Google".
18. In Google Tasks, make one task titled "Wixen proof Google task".
19. Press Ctrl+Shift+3 for Calendar, then F5. F5 in Calendar syncs it now. The Google event should arrive.
20. Press Ctrl+Shift+2 for Contacts, then F5. The Google contact should arrive.
21. Press Ctrl+Shift+5 for Tasks, then F5. The Google task should arrive.
22. Press Ctrl+Shift+E for a new event. Type the title "Wixen proof event", choose one of your Google calendars for it, and press Alt+S, Save.
23. Press Ctrl+Shift+C for a new contact, with all five name parts: Prefix (Alt+P) "Dr.", Given name (Alt+G) "Wixen", Middle name (Alt+I) "Proof", Family name (Alt+M) "Contact", Suffix (Alt+X) "Jr.". Then OK. Listening item 92 rides here: Google should keep the prefix, the middle name and the suffix.
24. Press Ctrl+Shift+T for a new task. Type "Wixen proof task", choose one of your Google lists, and press Alt+S, Save.
25. If you have two Google task lists: on that task, press Ctrl+Shift+V, Move to, and choose the other Google list.
26. Open Tools, Sync Calendar.
27. Open Tools, Sync Contacts.
28. Open Tools, Sync Tasks.
29. In Google's own apps, find the event "Wixen proof event", the contact with its five name parts, and the task in the list it was moved to. Say what each shows.
30. Listening item 106, its Google half: in Calendar, delete "Wixen proof event" with Delete. Wixen Mail asks whether to delete it, and says "Undo brings it back until your next action." Choose Yes; Enter answers No to this question.
31. Open Tools, Sync Calendar.
32. Press Ctrl+Z. You should hear "Undid Delete on Wixen proof event."
33. Open Tools, Sync Calendar again. The event should come back at Google as a new event. Say whether it did.
34. If time allows, do steps 30 to 33 for the proof contact in Contacts and the proof task in Tasks.
35. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
