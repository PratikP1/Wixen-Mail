# Sitting 2, done first: copying and moving in Gmail, and undoing them

**Waiting since 14 October.** This sitting waits until two things are done: the fixes from
the audit of 14 October and for the four problems you reported that day, which are phase
13.2, and a new build made after them, which you install first. The agent then corrects this
sheet against that build before you start, so the build and the sentences named below may
change.

The sittings run in the order 2, 3, 1, 4. Sitting 1 needs your Google key,
and you said the key comes later, so this one comes first.

This sitting is the new build's first start. That start lets the two
deletes of 20 September go to Gmail. Then it copies and moves proof
messages within your Gmail account, online, offline and across a restart,
and undoes a move, a copy and a mark before and after the server has heard
of them. About 45 to 60 minutes. It does not need your Google key.

The agent corrects this sheet against the build you installed and reads
your profile before you start. If a sentence you hear differs from the one
quoted here, the build is right and the sheet is wrong: say what you heard.

## What every sitting shares

- **Proof messages only.** Every message you send and change today has a
  subject beginning "Wixen proof", so the agent may print it, and the folder
  is named "Wixen proof". Nothing else of yours is touched.
- **Nothing runs on this computer while you work.** The agent waits until
  you say you are done.
- **Turning the network off stops the agent too.** Several steps here take
  this computer offline with Airplane mode, your usual way. You work the
  sheet alone and tell the agent when you are done.
- **Gmail on the web or on your phone is the other client.** It does not
  count against Gmail's limit on mail programs; a second desktop mail
  program on the same account would, and could look like a failure of what
  is being tested.
- **A browser sign-in lasts seven days while your key is in Testing.** This
  sitting does not use one.

## What you need

- The build installed, from `14-INSTALLING-THE-BUILD.md`.
- Gmail open on the web or your phone.
- On Gmail a folder is a label, so a copy is a second label on one message.
  Deleting a copy can remove the original too. That is why only proof
  messages are used.

## Steps

1. Start Wixen Mail from the Start menu. This is the new build's first start: the first check runs and sends the two deletes of 20 September, the two messages you deleted that day, which you said to let go. Let it finish; there is nothing to press.
2. If there is no "Wixen proof" folder on the Gmail account yet: in the folder tree on that account, open File, New, Folder, type "Wixen proof", and press Enter.
3. Send yourself eleven messages, however is quickest (Ctrl+Shift+M for a new message, Ctrl+Enter to send), with the subjects "Wixen proof copy 1", "Wixen proof copy 2", "Wixen proof copy 3", "Wixen proof move 1" to "Wixen proof move 4", "Wixen proof undo 1" to "Wixen proof undo 3", and "Wixen proof mark 1".
4. Press F9, Check Mail, so the eleven arrive in Inbox.
5. Copy online: on "Wixen proof copy 1", press Ctrl+Shift+Y, Copy to Somewhere Else, choose "Wixen proof", and press Enter. You should hear that it was copied to Wixen proof, once, and the row stays.
6. Copy offline and back: turn Airplane mode on.
7. On "Wixen proof copy 2", press Ctrl+Shift+Y, choose the folder, and press Enter.
8. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
9. Copy offline across a restart: turn Airplane mode on.
10. On "Wixen proof copy 3", press Ctrl+Shift+Y, choose the folder, and press Enter.
11. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
12. Turn Airplane mode off, start Wixen Mail, and let the check finish.
13. Move online, listening item 66: on "Wixen proof move 1", press Ctrl+Shift+V, Move to, choose "Wixen proof", and press Enter. The row should leave at once and the next message be read.
14. Move offline and back: turn Airplane mode on.
15. On "Wixen proof move 2", press Ctrl+Shift+V, choose the folder, and press Enter.
16. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
17. Move offline across a restart: turn Airplane mode on.
18. On "Wixen proof move 3", press Ctrl+Shift+V, choose the folder, and press Enter.
19. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
20. Turn Airplane mode off, start Wixen Mail, and let the check finish.
21. Move changed meanwhile: turn Airplane mode on.
22. On "Wixen proof move 4", press Ctrl+Shift+V, choose the folder, and press Enter.
23. On your phone, in Gmail, put "Wixen proof move 4" in the Trash.
24. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9. Say what was said.
25. Listening item 65 and the alpha page's item 14: go to "Wixen proof copy 1" in Inbox and press Shift+Down twice. Listen for "selected" and the row read for each row added.
26. Press Shift+Up once. Listen for "not selected" for the row that leaves.
27. Press Shift+Down once.
28. Press M, Mark as Read or Unread. You should hear one sentence with the count, such as "3 messages marked read", once and nothing per message.
29. Press Ctrl+A. Your screen reader should say the count on its own.
30. Press Down, without a command, to leave the selection.
31. Undo before the server heard: turn Airplane mode on.
32. On "Wixen proof undo 1", press Ctrl+Shift+V, choose the folder, and press Enter.
33. Press Ctrl+Z.
34. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
35. Undo after the server heard: on "Wixen proof undo 2", press Ctrl+Shift+V, choose the folder, and press Enter.
36. Wait ten seconds.
37. The alpha page's item 20: open the Edit menu with Alt+E and listen to Undo's name. It should name the move and the message, such as "Undo Move to Wixen proof: Wixen proof undo 2".
38. Press Escape.
39. Press Ctrl+Z. You should hear "Undid Move to Wixen proof on Wixen proof undo 2." Say where the cursor landed.
40. Undo of a copy after the server heard. Read this first: on Gmail, taking the copy away may put the original in the Trash with it, and this step is where that is read. On "Wixen proof undo 3", press Ctrl+Shift+Y, choose the folder, and press Enter.
41. Wait ten seconds.
42. Press Ctrl+Z. Wixen Mail asks "Take away the copy of" the message, then "The one it was copied from stays where it is." Answer Yes.
43. Undo of a mark: on "Wixen proof mark 1", press M.
44. Wait ten seconds.
45. Press Ctrl+Z.
46. The alpha page's item 20, its second half: press Ctrl+Shift+5 for Tasks.
47. Press Ctrl+Shift+T for a new task. Type "Wixen proof undo task", leave the list it offers, and press Alt+S, Save.
48. Press Delete on "Wixen proof undo task". Wixen Mail asks whether to delete "Wixen proof undo task", and says "Undo brings it back until your next action." Choose Yes; Enter answers No to this question.
49. Open the Edit menu with Alt+E and listen to Undo's name. It should name the delete and the task.
50. Press Escape.
51. Press Ctrl+Z. You should hear "Undid Delete on Wixen proof undo task." Say where the cursor landed.
52. In Gmail on the web or your phone, say what holds what: the "Wixen proof" label, Inbox, the Trash, and whether "Wixen proof mark 1" is unread.
53. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
