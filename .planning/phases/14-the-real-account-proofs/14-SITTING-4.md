# Sitting 4, done last: two accounts, a Trash emptied at a server, and POP

**Waiting since 14 October.** This sitting waits until two things are done: the fixes from
the audit of 14 October and for the four problems you reported that day, which are phase
13.2, and a new build made after them, which you install first. The agent then corrects this
sheet against that build before you start, so the build and the sentences named below may
change.

The sittings run in the order 2, 3, 1, 4, so this one comes after Sitting 1.

This sitting adds the accounts you have, brings a second Google account's
calendars, contacts and tasks, copies and moves messages between two
accounts, empties a Trash at a server that is not Google's or Microsoft's,
and takes POP messages off this computer. About 60 to 80 minutes, then about
two minutes on the days a 15-day emptying and a one-day POP removal fall due.

The agent corrects this sheet against the build you installed and reads
your profile before you start. If a sentence you hear differs from the one
quoted here, the build is right and the sheet is wrong: say what you heard.

## Which parts you can do

Each part needs its own kind of account. Do the parts you have accounts
for; a part without its account is recorded as still open, not as failed.

| Part | What it needs | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| A | A second Google account, added to your project's Test users | Adding an account brings its calendars, contacts and tasks. It is also the second account for B |
| B | Any second account you can add: A's, or another | Copy and move across two accounts, and whether a large message arrives whole |
| C | An IMAP account run by neither Google nor Microsoft | The Trash emptied at the server, when Wixen Mail closes and after 15 days |
| D | A POP account, with Leave mail on the server on | A POP message taken off this computer is not downloaded again |

C and D can be one provider added twice, once as IMAP and once as POP, if
the provider offers both and the account editor takes a second account at
the same address. If it does not, D waits for another account.

## What every sitting shares

- **Proof messages only.** Everything you make today has a title or subject
  beginning "Wixen proof", so the agent may print it. Nothing else of yours
  is touched.
- **Nothing runs on this computer while you work.** The agent waits until
  you say you are done.
- **Turning the network off stops the agent too.** Several steps here take
  this computer offline with Airplane mode. You work the sheet alone and
  tell the agent when you are done.
- **Each provider's own web reader or phone app is the other client.** A
  second desktop mail program on the same account can meet Gmail's limit on
  connections and look like a failure of what is being tested.
- **A browser sign-in lasts seven days while your key is in Testing.** If
  Sitting 1 was more than seven days ago, a sync of your first Gmail account
  may say its sign-in has run out; press Alt+T on it in the Account Manager
  again. Only Test users can sign in, so the second Google account goes on
  that list first.
- **You type every password yourself.** The agent never handles one.

## What you need

- The build installed, from `14-INSTALLING-THE-BUILD.md`, and your Google
  key placed.
- The accounts for the parts you choose, with their app passwords or
  passwords to hand.
- For B, a file of your own of about 10 MB that holds nothing private.

## Steps

1. Start Wixen Mail and let the first check finish.
2. Part A, before adding: in the second Google account's own Contacts, make one contact named "Wixen proof second contact".
3. In the second Google account's own Tasks, make one task titled "Wixen proof second task".
4. In your Google project, add the second Google account under Test users, as Help's page "Calendars, contacts and tasks for a Gmail account" describes.
5. Part A: press Ctrl+Shift+A to open the Account Manager.
6. Choose Add Account (Alt+A).
7. Fill in the second Google account: its address, its app password, and IMAP for how to read your mail. Then OK. Say what was said about the three modules when the account was added.
8. With the new account chosen in the Account Manager's list, press Alt+T, Sign In for Calendars, Contacts and Tasks. You should hear: "Signing in to Google for this account's calendars, contacts and tasks. In the browser, choose second-address@gmail.com, and finish there.", with the second account's Username where second-address@gmail.com stands.
9. In the browser, sign in with the second Google account, continue past Google's screen saying it has not verified this app, allow Google to share your name and email address with Wixen Mail, and then allow the three permissions.
10. Back in Wixen Mail you should hear: "Signed in to Google for this account's calendars, contacts and tasks. They are brought when you close the Account Manager."
11. Press Alt+C, Close. The three syncs run once. Say what was said.
12. Part A: tell the agent how many calendars the second Google account has.
13. Part B: send yourself, to the first Gmail account, six messages "Wixen proof across 1" to "Wixen proof across 6" (Ctrl+Shift+M for a new message, Ctrl+Enter to send). Give one of them, titled "Wixen proof across big", your 10 MB file as an attachment.
14. Press F9, Check Mail.
15. Part B, copy across online: on "Wixen proof across 1", press Ctrl+Shift+Y, Copy to Somewhere Else, choose a folder of the other account, and press Enter.
16. Part B, copy across offline across a restart: turn Airplane mode on.
17. Copy "Wixen proof across 2" to the other account the same way.
18. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
19. Turn Airplane mode off, start Wixen Mail, and let the checks finish.
20. Part B, move across online, listening item 67: on "Wixen proof across 3", press Ctrl+Shift+V, Move to, choose a folder of the other account, and press Enter. Listen for the row leaving, the cursor read on the next message, and the line shown, which names the folder and the account, such as "Moved to Inbox in" the other account's name.
21. Part B, move across offline across a restart, item 67's restart part: turn Airplane mode on.
22. Move "Wixen proof across 4" to the other account the same way.
23. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
24. Turn Airplane mode off, start Wixen Mail, and let the checks finish.
25. Part B, the first of three questions: copy "Wixen proof across big" to the other account.
26. Part B, the other two: in the other account, copy "Wixen proof across 5" into the first Gmail account's "Wixen proof" folder.
27. Copy the same message there a second time.
28. Part B, a message over 25 MB: only if an account already holds such a message, move it to the other account and say what was said. Wixen Mail cannot make one, because its composer and Gmail both stop at 25 MB.
29. Part B, optional: start copying "Wixen proof across big" to the other account, and turn Airplane mode on before it finishes.
30. Turn Airplane mode off, then press F9.
31. Part B: in each provider's own web reader or phone app, say where each "across" message is, its date, and whether "Wixen proof across big" opens with its attachment whole.
32. Part C: press Ctrl+Shift+A, choose Add Account (Alt+A), and fill in the IMAP account.
33. On the second page, press Alt+Y, Empty the Trash, and choose When Wixen Mail closes. Then OK, and Alt+C, Close.
34. Part C: send the IMAP account two messages, "Wixen proof trash 1" and "Wixen proof trash 2".
35. Press F9.
36. Press Delete on each of the two, so they are in that account's Trash.
37. Part C: press Ctrl+Q to quit.
38. In that provider's own web reader, say whether its Trash is empty.
39. Part C: start Wixen Mail.
40. Press Ctrl+Shift+A, choose the IMAP account, press Alt+E, Edit, Alt+N, Next, then Alt+Y, Empty the Trash, and choose After 15 days. Then OK, and Alt+C, Close.
41. Send that account "Wixen proof trash 3", press F9, and press Delete on it. The agent writes down the day it falls due.
42. Part D: press Ctrl+Shift+A, choose Add Account (Alt+A), and fill in the POP account's first page, then Alt+N, Next.
43. On the second page, press Alt+R, How to read your mail, and choose POP.
44. Press Alt+L to tick Leave mail on the server after downloading it.
45. Press Alt+D and type 1 for the days. Then OK, and Alt+C, Close.
46. Part D: send the POP account four messages, "Wixen proof pop 1" to "Wixen proof pop 4".
47. Press F9.
48. Part D, listening item 182: press Delete on all four, so they go to the Trash every POP account shares.
49. Go to that Trash in the folder tree, and open Action, This Folder, Empty Folder (Alt+A, T, Y). Listen to the question to its end. You should hear: "Empty Trash? 4 messages stored on this computer. They will be taken off this computer for good, and Wixen Mail keeps no other copy."
50. Press Enter. Enter should answer No, and nothing should go.
51. Part D, listening item 175: open Empty Folder again and answer Yes.
52. Press F9. The four should not come down again.
53. Part D, listening item 174's POP half: send the POP account "Wixen proof pop 5".
54. Press F9.
55. Press Delete on "Wixen proof pop 5".
56. Press Ctrl+Shift+A, choose the POP account, press Alt+E, Edit, Alt+N, Next, then Alt+Y, Empty the Trash, and choose When Wixen Mail closes. Then OK, and Alt+C, Close.
57. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
58. Start Wixen Mail.
59. Press F9. "Wixen proof pop 5" should not come down again.
60. Press Ctrl+Q to quit.
