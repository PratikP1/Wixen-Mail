# Phase 13.4 Plan 03: The text reader leaves out what a sender hid Summary

The text reader, the window `Enter` opens on a message, now reads an HTML message without the
blocks its sender hid and without a preview line's invisible padding, at any depth, and says
"1 block the sender did not show was left out." (or the plural) above the words when a block of
words went, exactly as the formatted view does. A conversation and Space read through the same
`body_text`, so they do too.

## What changed

- `src/presentation/reader_text.rs`, `body_text`: `drop_what_the_sender_hid(trimmed)` runs
  first; the converter and the parse-failure fallback both get the dropped markup. After the
  unchanged depth line, when `what_was_left_out(blocks_left_out)` is not empty, the sentence and
  a blank line go in front of the text and every heading landmark from the converter moves down
  by that prefix's length in characters. The signature landmark and the links are placed later
  from the final text.
- `src/application/hidden_text.rs`: the module doc names the text reader as the third place the
  drop runs.
- `tests/what_the_sender_hid_is_not_read.rs`: five cases under a new heading, and the header's
  "What this holds" names them.
- `guards/guards.toml`: four new records, all with `suite = "what_the_sender_hid_is_not_read"`;
  the five records the count check flagged re-measured; "what a sender hid is dropped from
  markup nested past the limit" now also names the text reader's depth case. Arrived-since
