<objective>
Say once for each draft that the server would not take it, until a save of that draft reaches the
server, instead of once a minute for as long as somebody writes while the server cannot be
reached.

Purpose: ledger 941, opened by 13.3-21 (premise 5 there). While the mail server cannot be
reached, `spawn_draft_append` answers `NotFiledAndTheOlderOneIsStillThere` at every automatic
save, and `Filed::needs_saying_for` says both refusals every time, so a person writing a long
message on a train hears "The draft is saved on this computer. The server would not take it..."
every minute, read aloud over what they are writing. Guardrail 5 asks for feedback that is
bounded. Pratik said yes on 2026-10-23 to the recommendation, the same once-per-draft rule
13.3-21 gave the older-copy sentence, until a save of that draft is taken (D-01).

Output: the rule, `Replaced`'s sentence reworded to name the server, four cases taken red first,
the guard records, the comments, the changelog
with dated corrections of 13.3-21's sentences, ledger 941 closed and three rows opened,
FOUND-04's dated line with Pratik's answer to 13.3-21's open choice, the four marks, and the pull
request.
</objective>
