# Phase 13.4 Plan 10: The browser watch retired Summary

Compose and Preview Before Send are destroyed the moment they close at wxWidgets 3.3.4, the
watch that kept them hidden until WebView2 reported is deleted with every use of it, and ledger
466 is fixed; ledger 661 turned out not to be 466's cause and stays open.

## What works now

- `show_compose_dialog_full` ends with `dialog.destroy()` and `show_send_preview` with
  `dlg.destroy()`. Neither widgets struct carries a watch, and `src/presentation/browser_ready.rs`
  is gone with its line in `mod.rs`. `git grep -n -E 'BrowserReady|browser_ready|destroy_when_ready' -- src tests`
  exits 1, and `git ls-files src/presentation/browser_ready.rs` prints nothing.
- `test_the_two_dialogs_holding_a_browser_are_destroyed_when_they_close` reads both bodies for the
  direct destroy of their own dialog and for no `when_ready(` hand-over, and gathers every miss
  before failing so the message names both functions. Red at `d3055821` with all four lines:
  each function "never destroys" its dialog and each "still hands" it to a watch.
- The closing test's live half builds the real composer through `build_compose_dialog`, sets a
  page, destroys the dialog at once, then makes a second browser on the frame and keeps the loop
  alive until it has reported plus one second. By hand after the green, `--nocapture`: exit 0,
  "the loop stayed alive 1267 ms after the composer was destroyed".
- 13.4-09's two crash tests, `test_a_browser_window_destroyed_while_it_is_made_does_not_end_the_process`
  and `test_ending_the_program_while_a_browser_is_made_does_not_crash_it`, ran green in the hook of
  `b2d874eb` (their file changed, a doc comment only).
