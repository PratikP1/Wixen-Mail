# Phase 13.4 Plan 11.1: The comments that name the old toolkit Summary

Every comment that described wxdragon 0.9.17 or wxWidgets 3.3.2 as the toolkit in use now names
0.9.23 or 3.3.4, after each statement was read in that release's source and found still true,
and every wxWidgets and wxdragon-sys line a comment cites points where it says at 3.3.4 and
0.9.23. Comments only, plus one test's failure text.

## Premises taken again

- 13.4-11 is merged (`7d8b514e` on `main`); the branch was cut from it.
- `target/debug/wxWidgets/include/wx/version.h` reads `wxMINOR_VERSION 3`,
  `wxRELEASE_NUMBER 4`. The cargo registry holds wxdragon and wxdragon-sys at 0.9.17, 0.9.22 and
  0.9.23. wxWidgets 3.3.2's source is no longer on disk, so the eight files the citations name
  were fetched read-only from `raw.githubusercontent.com/wxWidgets/wxWidgets/v3.3.2/src/...` into
  the scratchpad, and each cited 3.3.2 range was carried to 3.3.4 by aligning the two files with
  Python's `difflib.SequenceMatcher`, every line of the range required to match.
- Premise 3's commands on `main` at `7d8b514e`: the version command named 63 lines, the citation
  command 32. On the branch after both commits: 41 and 33. The 22 version lines that moved are in
  the table below; the 41 left are listed under "Kept".
- Premise 4: the record anchored at `page_focus.rs:147` was retired by 13.4-09 and is gone. A
  Python reading over every removed line of both commits against every record's `before` and
  `after` (1,755 records) found none holding an edited line, and the hook's one-place check passed
  on both commits. `wx_settings.rs`'s record anchors below the edited comment and was not touched.
- 13.4-11's addition to premise 2, the composer's three toolbar lines, was read against the
  `Deferred::Toolbar` arm, which always focuses the first button: they described neither arm, and
  the fact they stated, the group named on every arrival, already has its comment above
