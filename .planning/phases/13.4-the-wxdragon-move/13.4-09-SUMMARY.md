# Phase 13.4 Plan 09: The move to wxDragon 0.9.23 Summary

Wixen Mail builds and passes its whole suite on wxDragon 0.9.23 and wxWidgets 3.3.4, with the
webview feature and the crate's defaults only. List cells read back whole, tree rows free their
data, and a browser torn down while WebView2 makes it no longer ends the process, each held by a
test that was red at 0.9.17. The crate now builds on Linux and macOS; its tests do not pass there
yet.
