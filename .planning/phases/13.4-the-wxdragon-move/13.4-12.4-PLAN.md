<objective>
Put "Don't ask me again" on every question with two answers, stop a question somebody ticks it on,
and let Settings bring any stopped question back.

Purpose: the second half of Pratik's answer of 2026-10-25, "also include a 'do not ask again'
checkbox in order to ensure that the user is not bothered by them in the future" (D-02). A
question asked every time is one people learn to answer without hearing; one they can stop is
one they keep listening to. Every stop is a setting a person can see and undo, because
`CLAUDE.md` requires every setting to be reachable from Settings and a stop nobody can take back
is a command that silently changed.

Output: the check box in the box, the remember rule, the typed setting, the Questions page, the
save that keeps a stop made inside Settings, the one missing sentence, two window tests, the NVDA
case for the tab row, the records, the documents, ledger 967 closed and the new rows, the marks,
and the pull request.
</objective>
