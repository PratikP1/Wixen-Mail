<objective>
Retire the watch that holds Compose and Preview Before Send open until their browsers report,
now that the toolkit no longer crashes when such a window goes early.

Purpose: since 2026-09-15 the two windows holding a browser have been hidden at once and
destroyed only after WebView2 reported, because wxWidgets 3.3.2 delivered the browser's creation
reply to a control that had gone and Windows ended the program (ledger 466). wxdragon 0.9.23,
which 13.4-09 moved to, builds wxWidgets 3.3.4, which fixes that (wxWidgets #26491). Ledger 466
says the watch can be retired at that point. Pratik decided on 2026-10-20 to move wxDragon "and
all that implies", and taking out workarounds the new release makes unnecessary is part of it.

Output: the close of both windows at once, the watch module gone, the tests that leaned on it
moved off it, the waits that existed only for the crash gone from three more tests, two guard
records per close, ledger 466 closed and 661 measured, the changelog, three completion marks, and
a pull request.
</objective>
