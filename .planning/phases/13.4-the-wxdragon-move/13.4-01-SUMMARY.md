# Phase 13.4 Plan 01: A new contact Allow Changes holds back waits on the setting

With Allow Changes off, a contact typed here on a Google or Outlook account is now counted as
one change waiting on the setting at every contacts sync, and never as an error. The sync says
"1 change is waiting here: turn on Allow Changes in Settings to send it" instead of "1 error",
the log holds no error line for that contact, and the first contacts sync after the setting is
turned on makes the contact at the address book. The write gate still refuses before any
request is built. A create Google or Outlook itself turns down is still an error named by the
contact's id. Ledger 936, on Pratik's yes of 2026-10-23.

## What was done

- `sync_google_contacts` and `sync_microsoft_contacts`: an arm above each create loop's
  `Err(e) =>`, `Err(refused) if crate::service::outward::was_refused_by_the_gate(&refused)`,
  noting `local.id` in `result.waiting_on_the_setting`, under a one-line comment naming its
  address book. The error arms are unchanged.
- `ScriptedGoogle::create_contact` and `ScriptedMicrosoft::create_contact` answer
  `Error::Security(outward::refusal("add something to this account"))` when
  `the_account_is_read_only` is set, before recording anything in `sent`, the wording the real
  clients pass to `changing`. The field's doc says a change or a new contact.
- Two cases, `test_a_contact_typed_here_that_allow_changes_holds_back_waits_for_google` and
  `..._for_outlook`: a sync against a read-only address book leaves one change waiting, no
  error, nothing made, the Allow Changes sentence and no "error" in the summary, nothing sent
  and the contact still pending and known to no address book; a second sync against an address
  book that accepts makes her, one made and none waiting.
- Changelog entry under `[Unreleased]`, `### Fixed`; ledger 936 fixed, 959 opened for the
  tester, 953 dated with a second sighting; REAL-01's dated line and traceability row; the
  four marks.
