**4. Two mechanisms are needed, not one, and the research names only the
first.** Microsoft's `LOCALE_SMONTHNAME` page says a month name looked up on its
own is "the standalone, or nominative, form", and that to get the genitive form
an application "calls GetDateFormat or GetDateFormatEx with a date picture of
ddMMMM". Its `GetDateFormatEx` page says the genitive is retrieved "when the
date picture contains both a numeric form of the day (either d or dd) and the
full month name (MMMM)".

So: a date with a day in it wants `GetDateFormatEx` and a picture. A bare list
of twelve month names in a Choice, where there is no day, wants the standalone
form, which is `GetLocaleInfoW` with `LOCALE_SMONTHNAME1` to `12`. Using the
picture for the Choice puts Russian and Polish months in the wrong case in a
list, and using the lookup for a date puts them in the wrong case in a sentence.
