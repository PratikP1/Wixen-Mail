## Before any change: what the failing children build

Read from premise 2's lines before anything changed. The marker and signature window tests build `wx_compose::build_compose_dialog` under a hidden `Frame`, whose browser is `WebView::builder(&dialog)` in a `Dialog` (`wx_compose.rs:1091`), and they need the page: every step types into it. The invitation and meeting tests build `ReaderWindow::new` and the conversation page, a `Frame`. The candidates that difference allowed: the `Dialog` parent, the hidden `Frame`, or the WebView2 profile folder.

The difference turned out not to be the cause. In the same run the meeting target's formatted-window cases finished at 20:06:57, 30.6 s after the cases before them, and its `GIVE_UP_AFTER_TICKS` is half a minute: its child never saw the browser's windows either, and passed because its cases do not need the page. Every child's browser failed on the runner; only the two readings that type into a page depend on it.
