# Phase 13.1 Plan 04: Imports say what they did Summary

A saved Outlook message of class `IPM.Note.SMIME` is counted as arrived
encrypted and both imports say how many; a folder or zip's folders are made
only when a message is filed into them, the filling moved into the
application layer; and the folder import says its already-present,
unreadable and unsaved counts in the single-file import's words.

## What works now

- `one_saved_message::read` sets `arrived_encrypted` for the class
  `IPM.Note.SMIME` in any capitals, and not for
  `IPM.Note.SMIME.MultipartSigned` or `IPM.Note`. The encrypted part comes in
  as the message's attached file. Both imports' closing sentences say "1
  message arrived encrypted, with its words in the encrypted part attached to
  it" after the signature sentence.
- `fill_folders_from` in `wx_app.rs` keeps its dispatch on `what_was_chosen`
  and calls `importing_messages::an_archive_brought_in`
  (`src/application/importing_messages.rs:1022` at `c6481c7c`) for
  `AnArchive`. Each planned folder is read through
  `AFolderMadeAtItsFirstMessage::found` (`:924`), which uses `get_folder`
  and makes nothing, and made at its first filed message. A folder of old
  Word documents leaves nothing under Imported; an archive imported twice
  says "No folders were imported" and what was already there.
- `FoldersImported` carries `already_here`, `could_not_be_read` and
  `not_written_down`, and `what_was_not_brought_in` (`:807`) says them for
  both imports. The single-file sentences are unchanged: the four cases
  quoting them (`test_messages_the_folder_already_held_are_counted_out_loud_rather_than_passed_over`,
  `test_one_message_already_here_is_said_in_the_singular`,
  `test_a_part_of_the_file_that_could_not_be_read_is_said_in_the_readers_own_words`,
  `test_a_message_that_could_not_be_saved_is_said_rather_than_left_to_the_log`)
  pass as they were.
- `grep -n 'a_folder_for_imported_mail' src/presentation/wx_app.rs` lists
  one line, 17587, inside `fn one_file_of_mail_brought_in(` (line 17572),
  and none inside `fill_folders_from`.
