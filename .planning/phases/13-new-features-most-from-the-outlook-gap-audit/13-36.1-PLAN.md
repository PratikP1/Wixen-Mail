  mark whose signature part was never kept says "This message carries a

- **D5 (the planner's, the brief's question):** the moment is kept in the
  mail database, as one row of a table of its own, `last_message_before`
  (`what TEXT PRIMARY KEY, message_id INTEGER NOT NULL, noted_at TEXT NOT
  NULL`), and never in `AppConfig`. Three reasons. It describes the
  messages in that database, so it has to live and die with them: delete
  the database and a fresh one starts with nothing before its mark; copy
  the database to another computer and its mark still describes its own
  messages, where a setting would stay behind or be missing. A setting is a
  choice a person makes, and nobody should choose this: a field no screen
  offers would fail `test_every_setting_somebody_can_change_is_offered_by_a_screen`
  or join `STORED_AND_OFFERED_BY_NOTHING`, the exception list `CLAUDE.md`
  says must shrink, and "a setting nobody sees is not a setting". And
  `work_done_once` (`mod.rs:2494`) was the nearest shape and was not taken:
