<objective>
The tests that send keys to plain controls stop failing when somebody is
at the keyboard, and stop putting windows in front of that person, by
building their windows on a desktop made for the run, where no input and no
other run can reach them.

Purpose: Pratik said "yes to moving the plan as well as others." on
2026-10-02, answering whether the tests that type keys should get the same
treatment as the clipboard test. Key-posting cases stopped the merges of
13-44 (five targets, the minute after an unlock while somebody was typing)
and 13-44.5 (a_move_completes_here_first's Enter case), and ledger 580
records mark_as_read failing while the tester used the machine. This plan
moves the ten plain-control targets and the directory sign-in, and adds the
census that keeps every future key sender off the interactive desktop.
13-44.6.3, which runs straight after, moves the five targets that need a
process of their own on a desktop (a browser in the window, or a reader in
another process).

Output: eleven test files on desktops of their own, the census, two guard
records, ledger 580 closed and three entries recorded with counts, FOUND-23's
dated line, the marks.
</objective>
