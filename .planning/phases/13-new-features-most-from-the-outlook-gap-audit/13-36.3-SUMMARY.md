# Phase 13 Plan 36.3: One day of a repeating meeting, changed by its organiser Summary

**An organiser's update for one day of a repeating meeting now moves that day alone,
kept apart from the series and linked to its meeting and its day; a cancellation of one
day offers Remove from Calendar for that day, which calls it off the series; a message
naming no day changes the whole meeting; and the invitation's sentence names the day.**
On a calendar server and a calendar kept here. On Google, Outlook and a calendar only
read, the change is said and not applied, with the calendar named.

## What runs now

Opening such a message in the text reader or the formatted window goes through
`reading_a_message::what_opening_it_changed_among`, which asks
`one_day_of_a_series::what_the_calendar_holds_for_that_day`, hands the answer to
`meeting_changes::what_opening_it_changes`, and on `MoveOneDay` saves the day, links it,
then takes it off the series. The new target reads that from the bytes of a raw message
to the rows the calendar panel shows, and presses the text reader's real button through
`remove_now` into the main window's real handler.
