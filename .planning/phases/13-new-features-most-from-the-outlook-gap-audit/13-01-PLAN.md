<objective>
Edit, Undo and Edit, Redo on the main window's Edit menu, acting on the
focused text box through the box's own undo, greyed when there is
nothing to undo so the menu reads its state, and always speaking when
pressed.

Purpose: #47, the tester on 2026-09-15: "There are no general undo/redo
commands that provide corresponding functionality." GAP-02's first
half: "Edit, Undo and Redo for the focused control on every surface
first." This plan gives the main window its Undo and Redo on the
native control's one step; 13-05 replaces the one step with a history
of several steps in the main window's boxes, 13-06 carries the history
into every dialog, and 13-07 to 13-09 give Undo its meaning in the
lists. Nothing in this plan reaches a server.

Output: the rule in `application::editing`, the Win32 transport in a
new `presentation::text_undo`, the two items and their handling in the
main window, Undo Send moved to third with a new letter, a built
target, the Undo Send reading rewritten, the pages, the changelog, the
ledger.
</objective>
