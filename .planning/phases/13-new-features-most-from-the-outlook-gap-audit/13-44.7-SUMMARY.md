# Phase 13 Plan 44.7: The Trash emptied as Wixen Mail closes, and on POP accounts Summary

Empty the Trash has its fourth answer, When Wixen Mail closes: on a real close the window goes,
then each account set that way has its Trash emptied within one five-second limit for all of
them, POP first, then the sign-off as before. POP accounts are offered all four answers and
have their share of the shared Trash emptied on this computer only. A POP message deleted here
is no longer downloaded again by the next check (ledger 753).

## What works now

- **The close** (`emptying_the_trash::empty_on_the_way_out`, called from
  `wx_app::emptying_the_trash_on_the_way_out`). In the `LetItClose` arm, after the new
  `frame.show(false)` and before `signing_off`. One `tokio::time::Instant` deadline, five
  seconds (`LONG_ENOUGH_TO_EMPTY_THE_TRASH`), read before each message and bounding the session
  open and every delete with `timeout_at`. IMAP messages go through
  `moves_waiting::delete_here_then_at_the_server`, the step the menu's Delete in the Trash and
  a rule's Delete share, on `mail_session::the_session_at`. Who empties is
  `who_empties_the_trash(account.protocol(), WhoRunsTheMail::of(account))`. One log line per
  account through `what_the_close_did_for_the_log`: the account and three counts, no subject.
- **What the limit leaves.** `test_closing_stops_at_the_deadline_leaving_one_waiting_and_the_rest_untouched`
  proves it with a double whose second delete is never answered: the first message is deleted
  here and not waiting, the second is deleted here and in `moves_waiting` (the future dropped
  at the deadline leaves the shared step's waiting row, which the next start's replay sends
  before it lists anything), the third is untouched and still stamped in the Trash, and the
  pass returns inside the limit plus a one-second margin.
- **At a check.** An account set to empty on close is never emptied at a check (D23); its
  check says once a day "Nothing will be emptied from Trash in Work when Wixen Mail closes,
  because Allow Changes does not let this account change mail." or the no-Trash form (D26).
- **POP** (`empty_the_trash_here_at_a_pop_check`, called in `check_pop_mail` after
  `ensure_local_folders` and before `MailController::new()`). The shared Trash is found through
  `local_folders::local_trash` and `stored_under`; `what_this_account_put_in_the_shared_trash`
  reads only this account's rows; each goes through `local_delete::perform`, so the row is
  marked deleted here and keeps its identifier and download time. Once a day, at most 500. The
  sentence: "Emptied 1 message of Old ISP's from the Trash on this computer that had been
  there more than 30 days." With deleting here off: "Nothing was emptied from the Trash on this
  computer for Old ISP." followed by `DELETING_IS_SWITCHED_OFF`.
- **The owner record** (`messages.rs`, `move_message_from`). After the three lines premise 6
  names, which are word for word as they were, an ordinary move works out
  `the_account_leaving_for_a_shared_folder` and writes it, through the merge's `CASE`, only
  while the origin columns are empty. `pop_uidls_for_account` and
  `pop_download_times_for_account` read `(f.account_id = ?1 OR m.original_account_id = ?1)`.
- **The editor.** `show_who_empties_the_trash` shows the choice on a POP account's page, four
  entries, and never the no-Trash line there. `Alt+Y` on the POP page in
  `docs/KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md`; the POP page's letters are P, O, F, L, D and C beside the common
  ones, and the dialog test reads them as one per control.
- **The description** (`allowed::EMPTYING_THE_TRASH_IS_EXPERIMENTAL`) says what a POP account's
  emptying and the close do (D25), and nothing about the text, which is 13-44.8's (D24).

Reachability: the window's close reaches the pass for every account; `check_pop_mail` reaches
the POP emptying; the Account Manager's Edit reaches the choice on both protocols. What CI and
the scan show is in the pull request's checks, read before the merge. Nothing has met a real
server: ledger 784.

is a dead-code refusal from clippy, the finding 13-44.6 recorded. The call sat before the

message names them: the planted companion, the check-leaves-alone case (taken red by its
record's break) and the provider census, whose new caller existed.

**2. [Shape] A session seam of its own, and the whole Account on the close's type.**
`OpensASession` requires a session that answers for crossings, which the test double does not;
`OpensTheSessionToEmpty::Session` is `Deref<Target: ReplaysAMove + Sized>`, an `Arc` in the
program and an `Rc` in the cases. The plan's `AnAccountToEmpty` listed id, name, protocol and
`allow_deleting_here`; `local_delete::perform` takes an `&Account`, so a refactor commit before
task 2's red made it carry the account.

**3. [Shape] The POP check is its own function**, `empty_the_trash_here_at_a_pop_check`,
rather than a branch of `empty_at_a_check`: it needs no server, and 13-44.6's reading holds
`empty_at_a_check(` to one line, the IMAP check's.

**4. [Rule 1] The flag-change census refused task 1's first green.** Its list of callers of
`delete_here_then_at_the_server` gained `empty_one_imap_trash_on_the_way_out`, with the reason
in its comment; the window's reading holds that caller to the real close.

**5. [Measurement] The policy case passed at task 2's first red.** The store stamps a download

policy removed that copy, which hid the half the case was about. The premise held; the case
now reads `pop_download_times_for_account` before the check runs, and was red at the commit.

**6. [Shape] Two guard breaks differ from the plan's words.** "Only a real close empties the
Trash" drops the hide rather than moving the call into the hiding arm, since a record is one
contiguous edit; the companion case plants the move. "A POP account empties only its own
messages" is on `in_the_trash.rs`, where the owner is read, not on the module.

**7. [Rule 2] Allow Changes gates only the close's IMAP half**, and the POP page never shows
the no-Trash line, which reads the server's folders.

**8. [Process] Task 2's green was refused once by `the_planning_files_agree_with_themselves`**,
because the four marks were already made in the working tree and the hook reads it; this
