<objective>
Synced events carry their iCalendar UID and organiser, so an invitation can
find the meeting a provider already put on the calendar.

Purpose: the research's second finding. An invitation's UID matches
nothing a Google or Microsoft sync wrote, because the sync stores the
provider's own event id and nothing in `src/` reads Google's `iCalUID` or
Graph's `iCalUId`. Without this, 13-13's update and cancellation cannot find
the meeting on those accounts, and an answered meeting is filed twice
(ledger 154). The organiser is carried for 13-13's rule that only the
organiser on the calendar's copy may move or cancel a meeting.

Output: two columns, the lookups, the four sources, the answer's lookup,
the changelog, the ledger.
</objective>
