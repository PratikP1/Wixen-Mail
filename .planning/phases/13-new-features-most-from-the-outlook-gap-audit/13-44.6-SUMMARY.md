# Phase 13 Plan 44.6: An IMAP account's Trash emptied after 15 or 30 days Summary

An IMAP account that the one check of 13-44.5 does not call Gmail or Microsoft can be set on
`Alt+Y` in the account editor to empty its Trash after 15 or 30 days. At its first whole-account
check of each day, what went into its Trash on this computer that long ago is taken off the server
the way the menu's Delete takes it off inside the Trash, at most 500, with one sentence; a Gmail or
Microsoft account sees a line saying its provider does it.

## What works now

- **The record** (`data::message_cache::in_the_trash`). A table, `in_the_trash_since`, kept by four
  triggers on `messages`: a row inserted into a folder typed Trash, or moved into one from a folder
  that is not, is stamped; moved out of every Trash, or deleted, it loses its stamp; renumbered
  within a Trash it keeps it. Every open stamps the rows already in a Trash with none, so a message
  there before this build counts from the first open. `what_has_been_in_the_trash` leaves out rows
  marked deleted, rows filed here and rows with a waiting move (D16). `trash_last_emptied` holds the
  day each account's Trash last had its turn. Both additive.
- **The emptying** (`application::emptying_the_trash::empty_at_a_check`). Called once, in
  `spawn_mail_sync` after the folder loop and before the tree is read back, for a check of the whole
  account. Never, or an account its provider empties, does nothing and uses no day; the day already
  used does nothing; an account that has never listed its folders says nothing; no Trash recognised
  and Allow Changes closed each say one sentence and use the day; otherwise each message due, oldest
  first, at most 500, goes through `moves_waiting::delete_here_then_at_the_server` as
  `DeleteOutright` from the Trash, and an unreachable server leaves the one under way waiting and the
  rest untouched.
- **The sentence.** "Emptied 12 messages from Trash in Work that had been there more than 30 days."
  with a clause each for messages kept waiting, put back and why, and left for the next day, on
  `UIUpdate::TheTrashWasEmptied`, shown on the status bar and said at Normal on the topic "trash".
  The log names the account and counts, never a subject.
- **The setting.** `AppConfig.trash_emptying`, read through `trash_emptying_for`, written through
  `set_trash_emptying_for`, which keeps no row for Never; named in `OFFERED_BY_ANOTHER_SCREEN`.
- **The editor.** "Empt&y the Trash (experimental):" and its choice after "En&able this account",
  described by `allowed::EMPTYING_THE_TRASH_IS_EXPERIMENTAL`, opening on the stored answer and
  written back on OK. `show_who_empties_the_trash` decides, on the connection page of an IMAP
  account, the choice or the provider's line, through `WhoRunsTheMail::from_what_is_known` with the
  IMAP server box and the address box; it is called when the page opens and from the protocol and
  IMAP server handlers. `say_whether_the_trash_is_recognised` adds the no-Trash line, called by
  `show_edit` from the account's stored folders.
- **The shared step.** A rule's Delete and an emptying both go through
  `delete_here_then_at_the_server`, moved out of 13-44.3's `carry_out_the_deletes` in a refactor
  commit before the first red; `what_a_rules_delete_calls_for` is now
  `what_a_waiting_delete_calls_for`.

Reachability: the window's check reaches the emptying for every IMAP account set to a number of
days; the Account Manager's Edit reaches the choice. What CI and the scan show is in the pull
request's checks, read before the merge. No real mail server has been emptied: ledger 771.

Microsoft's page: `https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/recover-and-restore-deleted-items-in-outlook-49e81f3c-c8f4-4426-a0b9-c0fd751d48ce`,
read on 2026-10-02, under "Recover items from your Deleted Items or Junk Email folders in
Outlook.com and Outlook on the web": "Email is automatically deleted from your Deleted Items folder
after 30 days." So the Microsoft line keeps the number and no ledger `todo` is opened for it.
