<objective>
PGP signatures checked and said.

Purpose: GAP-05's "a PGP signature is verified and said", #52 points 3 and
6, and ledger 497. Today a PGP signature is always "This message carries a
PGP signature, which Wixen Mail cannot check" (`reader_text.rs:526`), and a
PGP/MIME signed message's bytes are not even kept, because
`claims_a_signature` is S/MIME's alone and says why. 13-16 and 13-17 put
public keys here; this plan uses them, with the same discipline S/MIME's
sentences keep: never "signed" alone, and never a sentence that could be
heard as "genuine" when it means "unchecked".

Output: verification in `service::pgp`, the kept bytes, the verdicts and
their sentences, the stored-before sentence, the conversation's per-message
verdict, the limits rewritten, the guide, the changelog, the ledger.
</objective>
