step.

three boxes the Edit menu reaches.

<decisions date="2026-09-24">
Taken 2026-09-24 from the research's recommendation (RESEARCH-1,
section 3); Pratik may overrule.

- **Decision 8:** several steps of undo in every text box, in this
  phase: this plan for the main window, 13-06 for the dialogs.
- **Research's alternative, rejected unless Pratik prefers it:**
  switching every box to a Rich2 control, whose RichEdit has several
  steps of its own, changes the window class of about 35 controls and
  what MSAA and UI Automation expose for each.
- **Discretion taken here:** a bound of 100 steps per box, so a long
  note cannot grow a history without limit; a step is a run of typing
  up to a word boundary (a space or punctuation), a paste, a cut, or a
  run of deleting in one direction.
</decisions>
