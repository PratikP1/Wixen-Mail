<objective>
Compose offers an account's other addresses and sends from the one
chosen, and the From list now decides who sends.

Purpose: the compose half of RESEARCH-3's C11. Planning it found a
defect beneath it: the From list today is an index into the accounts
that people lookup, the signature and the preview follow, while the send
and the draft use whichever account is open in the main window. So a
person who picks their second account in From hears its signature and
sees its address in the preview, and the message leaves from the first.
This plan makes the chosen entry the one answer for all of them, and
adds the other addresses 13-33 stores.

Output: the list built from entries, every consumer through the entry,
the send and the draft following the choice, the reading target, the
guide's section, the alpha page, the changelog and the ledger.
</objective>
