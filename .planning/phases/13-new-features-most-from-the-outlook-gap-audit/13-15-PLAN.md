<objective>
PGP/MIME mail opens.

Purpose: #52 point 2 and ledger 145. Only inline PGP, where the armour sits
in the body, reaches the opener today (`service/pgp/mod.rs:20-23` says so);
a `multipart/encrypted` message puts the armour in a separate part and is
never offered to the key. This plan marks it at arrival, offers the part
on opening, and parses what comes out in memory, reusing every rule 13-14
set for decrypted content.

Output: the header reading, the mark, the opening, the fixture, the
advisory entry, the pages, the changelog, the ledger.
</objective>
