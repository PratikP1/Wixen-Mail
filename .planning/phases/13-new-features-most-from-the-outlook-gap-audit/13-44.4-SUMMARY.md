# Phase 13 Plan 44.4: A colleague's own working hours Summary

A colleague Microsoft places is now judged by the days and hours set in their own Outlook, on
the clock they keep them on, to the minute, read from the getSchedule answer the event form
already asks for. Everybody else, the organiser included, keeps the working day set in
Settings on their own clock. No sentence changes its words; which times come first and whose
working day each is said to be outside of do.

## What was built

- **The read** (`src/service/free_busy.rs`). `TheirWorkingHours` reads `daysOfWeek`,
  `startTime` and `endTime` beside `timeZone`. `OneDiary::their_working_week` is pure and
  answers nothing where the zone cannot be placed, where any day word is not a day, where the
  list is empty or null, or where either time is not `%H:%M:%S%.f`; the days are collected
  through `Option<WeekdaySet>`, so one bad word refuses the whole list. `Heard.working_week`
  carries it; `one_answer_each` keeps the first week any place gave.
- **The judgement** (`src/application/when_people_are_free.rs`). `TheirWorkingWeek` on
  `Invited.working_week`. `the_hours_they_keep` picks a person's own week or, where there is
  none, `WorkingDay::on_the_clock_of(where_they_are(..))`, every day of the week. Both become
  one `HoursKept`, whose `hold_the_whole_of` replaced `falls_inside_the_working_day`: the
  span inside the shift on the clock face of the zone the hours are kept in, and the day the
  shift opened on among its days, a night shift belonging to the day it opens.
- **The organiser** (`src/application/asking_when_free.rs`). `your_own_diary` gives
  `working_week: None`, so the Settings day is theirs.
- **The pages.** The guide's "Where each guest is, and when they work" paragraph with Ada's
  New York week as the worked example, and the hand-built zone paragraph saying her hours are
  not used either; one sentence on the privacy page; the alpha page asks whether a time
  outside a colleague's Outlook hours was said as outside their working day; the changelog
  under Changed.

Reachability: the production path is unchanged, `managers.rs`'s worker calls
`free_busy::when_they_are_free`, which reads the week, and hands `found` to
`when_people_are_free::when_we_could_meet`, which judges by it.
