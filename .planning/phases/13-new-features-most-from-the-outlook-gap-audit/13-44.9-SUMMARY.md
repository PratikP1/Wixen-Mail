# Phase 13 Plan 44.9: What forgetting costs Summary

Measured, and built neither: after 13-44.8's taking off and the next check, secure delete off
(the program today) left words of 9 of 1,000 messages at 200,000 in unused space on pages of
`messages`, on and fast left 1 each at costs within 8% of off, VACUUM needed about twice the
file in extra disk, and the choice is Pratik's in ledger 788.

## What works now

- **The reading** (`what_forgetting_costs`, `#[cfg(test)]`, its line at the end of the module
  list in `mod.rs`). `copies_of` finds every offset of a word in the bytes of
  `message_cache.db` and `message_cache.db-wal`; `where_each_lies` names each offset's page,
  `offset / page_size + 1`, through `dbstat` read once into a map, or free. The planted mail is
  written through the store's own calls: the rest the IMAP way in batches of
  `INITIAL_FETCH_LIMIT` with each text, the planted the POP way, marked read and deleted into the
  shared Trash, then taken off through `local_delete::perform` and 13-44.8's path, then
  `let_the_search_index_forget_what_was_taken_off`.
- **The measurement**, two `#[ignore]` cases refusing a debug build, each pointing `TMP` and
  `TEMP` at a folder of its run and printing its rows; the 200,000 case refuses a drive with less
  free than three times the largest file the tester's size wrote, scaled.
- **Nothing in the program changes.** The module compiles only for tests; nothing is spoken or
  shown, no setting, no key, no schema change, and the branch was not pushed.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3] The planted messages number themselves through `next_uid_for_filing`.** The first
red commit was refused by `test_only_the_dispatcher_and_a_pop_inbox_ask_for_the_counting_up_numbering`,
which reads every file under `src` for a direct `next_local_uid` call and allows only the
dispatcher and `pop_sync`. For a POP inbox the dispatcher answers the same number.

**2. [Rule 1] The finding at `68f953f6` was not deterministic, corrected in `61a1636b`.** It
asserted no copy at all. A guard run reddened it under a break it has nothing to do with, and
fifteen runs alone failed eight times: one of 300 messages left one or two copies, an earlier
version of its row with its subject and snippet, in unused space on a page of `messages`. It now
asserts every copy lies there and at most 10 of 300 messages leave one, with the precondition
that all 300 had copies before; the next 12 runs passed. Because of it, four 13-44.8 records
gained the finding in their red lists, one record ("still stops the next check downloading it")
gained it through the flake and lost it again, and the eight records were measured a fourth time.
D42's "building either choice turns it red" holds only in part: secure delete on every write
would leave it green with nothing to count, so its doc comment tells whoever builds it to turn it
round.

**3. [Shape] The module's line is at the end of the list in `mod.rs`, not beside
`schema_template`'s,** because rustfmt orders the module declarations alphabetically, and it is
two lines, the attribute and the declaration, against the acceptance's one.

**4. [Shape] `measure` takes a `Shape`, the message count and how many are taken off,** so the
format case runs 120 messages and 30 taken off in about a second; the measurement passes 1,000.
`one_of_the_rest` takes the inbox's id rather than the folders, so the second connection on its
own thread holds no reference to the store.

**5. [Measurement] The second connection begins 1 ms after the command is let go,** not 20 ms,
because an incremental vacuum is over in a few milliseconds; its rows say when an attempt began
after the command finished. At 200,000 the read of 199,000 rows came first and took 2.20 s, so
the write began 2.21 s into the VACUUM; a write arriving at the start was not measured.

**6. [Brief] One read-only shell command carried an assignment named after a banned text tool,**
