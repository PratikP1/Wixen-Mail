<decisions date="2026-09-27">
- **D1 (Pratik's, 2026-09-27, item 2):** a Google, Outlook or calendar-server
  check that meets a meeting whose UID matches a row an answer filed merges
  the two rather than adding one.
- **D2 (the planner's, for him to overrule):** the merged meeting is the
  provider's copy, on the answer's row. The answer keeps its row, so its
  version, its word and its moment; its time-blocking stays; everything
  about the meeting itself (title, time, place, guests, repeat, status,
  calendar, identifier, version marker, link) is the provider's. Why: the
  provider's copy is the calendar's authority on the meeting, the invitation
  that filed the row came by mail from somebody who may not be the
  organiser, and the answer files a repeating meeting without its rule
  (premise 1, `:114`) while the provider's copy keeps it. What it costs: a
  title, place or category typed onto the answered meeting between the
  answer and its first calendar check is replaced by the provider's; the
  guide and the changelog say so.
- **D3 (the planner's):** the merged row waits to be sent only when the
  answer's time-blocking differs from the copy's. An Accept against a busy
  copy sends nothing; a Tentative or a Decline sends its time-blocking once,
  through the gated push. Why: every change sent to a guest's copy is ledger
  154's open question, and a row whose change a provider refuses waits for
  ever and stops taking the provider's later changes. On Google, which
  carries only busy or free (`transparency`), a Tentative goes up as busy
  and the next Google read writes busy on the row, because the held-row
  arm keeps category and status and not `show_as`
  (`application/calendar.rs:2975`, `:2619-2623`, `:1596-1612` on `main` at
  `fe9bd124`, and `:2976`, `:2620-2624` and `:1597-1613` at `85a5f873`,
  read and not run); the answer itself stays recorded as
  Tentative. Outlook keeps `showAs`, and a calendar server's read keeps
  the held row's `show_as` (`caldav_sync.rs:1284`). Added when the plans
  landed on 2026-09-29, from the checker's reading.
- **D4 (the planner's, 13-12's rule):** the organiser is the one the
  provider names; where it names nobody, the one the answer recorded stays,
  as `file_the_answer` falls back to the invitation's (`answered_meetings.rs:231-236`).
- **D5 (the planner's):** "a row an answer filed" is a row in this account
  whose provider identifier is still the meeting's UID, with no source,
  answered here (`answered_version` set), and not one day of a series
  (`cut_from_event_id` and `provider_recurrence_id` both unset). An answer
  writes the UID as the provider identifier on every new row (premise 1,
  `:99`), so the UID column is not needed to find it, and a row a provider
  has settled (a new identifier, or a source) is never taken.
- **D6 (the planner's):** a merge is counted as updated, because the
  calendar already held the meeting.
- **D7 (the planner's):** the merged row moves from My Calendar into the
  provider's calendar, where the provider's copy belongs and where a
  waiting change can be sent.
- **D8 (the planner's):** a published feed is not merged: it is a calendar
  somebody subscribed to, a change in it can never be sent, and its refresh
  replaces what it held.
- **D9 (the planner's):** a database where a check already filed the
  provider's copy beside an answer, before this build, keeps both rows. None
  is known (premise 8), and folding two existing rows is a deletion, which
  is a decision of its own; a ledger `todo` carries it.
</decisions>
