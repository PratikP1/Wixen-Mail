<decisions date="2026-09-24">
Taken 2026-09-24 from the research's recommendation (RESEARCH-1, section
3); Pratik may overrule.

- **Decision 2, plain text first:** the page carries the header lines
  and the words as the reader window shows them, without pictures or
  formatting. 13-03's guide and changelog say so.
- **Decision 4, the job's name:** plain and by kind, "Wixen Mail
  message", because Windows shows it in the print queue and a shared
  printer's queue is a room other people are in.
- **Decision 6, the printed size:** a fixed 11 point, no new setting.
  This plan's layout takes a measuring function and a line count, so the
  size lives in 13-03's transport alone.
- **Decision 7, what is said:** one sentence, "Sent Quarterly report to
  HP LaserJet 1022, 2 pages.", and nothing per page.
- **Discretion taken here:** a cancelled dialog says one sentence that
  nothing was printed, because a dialog closing in silence leaves a
  person unable to tell a cancel from a failure; a failure says what
  failed and what to try.
</decisions>
